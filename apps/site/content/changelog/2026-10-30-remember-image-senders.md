---
title: Load images once or always for a sender
date: 2026-10-30
summary: Load images now asks whether to load a sender's images just this once or always, and says when a sender can't be remembered.
---

- **Once or always.** When we can confirm who sent a message, **Load images** asks: **Just this once**, or **Always
  for** the sender's address, which loads their images by themselves from then on. **Stop loading from this sender**
  undoes it.
- **Why it asks again.** When we can't confirm a message really comes from the sender's domain, its images load this
  once and the note explains that we'll ask again next time, so a message pretending to be from someone can't learn
  when you open it.
- Your **Remote images** setting and loading images from contacts work as before. See
  [Loading images through our privacy proxy](/docs/webmail#loading-images-through-our-privacy-proxy).
