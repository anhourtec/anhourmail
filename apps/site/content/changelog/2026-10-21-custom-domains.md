---
title: Connect your own domain
date: 2026-10-21
summary: Plus and Supporter accounts can add a domain, see every DNS record it needs, and watch each one check out.
---

- **Add a domain** from the console's Domains page. You get the exact records to publish, each with a copy button
  and a short reason it's needed.
- **Live checks.** Every record shows whether it's found, missing or set to a different value. Press **Check now**
  any time; we also re-check pending domains every few minutes for their first week.
- **Verified means yours.** A domain becomes active only after its ownership record checks out, so nobody can claim
  a domain they don't control by adding it first.
- **Remove a domain** once no mailbox or alias uses it.
