---
title: Images in mail you sent
date: 2026-10-31
summary: Opening a message in your Sent folder no longer says we can't confirm who sent it.
---

- **Your own mail.** A message in your **Sent** folder (for example a reply quoting someone's newsletter) still keeps
  its remote images blocked until you choose **Load images**, which loads them this once. The note no longer says we
  can't confirm who sent it, and there's no question about remembering a sender: it's yours. See
  [Loading images through our privacy proxy](/docs/webmail#loading-images-through-our-privacy-proxy).
