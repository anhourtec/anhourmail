---
title: Webmail replies, suspensions and phone compose
date: 2026-10-28
summary: Quoted replies fold and keep their > lines, open conversations update, a suspended sign-in is explained, and phones get a New email button.
---

- **Quoted replies.** A rich reply's quote is marked as a quote, so mail apps fold it away, and the plain-text copy
  prefixes quoted lines with `>` under the "On … wrote:" line. See [Webmail](/docs/webmail#rich-text-and-html-email).
- **Open conversations update.** A reply that arrives while you're reading the conversation appears in it, and
  **Refresh** updates the open conversation too, keeping what you had expanded.
- **Suspensions are explained.** Someone whose sign-in Anhourtec suspended can't use the webmail either, and is told
  how to appeal. A suspended mailbox, one being deleted and one that no longer exists each get their own message. See
  [Webmail](/docs/webmail#sign-in).
- **Writing.** After you add a link, typing carries on after it instead of replacing it. Switching a formatted message
  to plain text asks first. Images from other servers show as placeholders while you write and in the Insert HTML
  preview, and are still sent.
- **Desktop notifications** explain how to allow them when the browser has blocked them.
- **Phones and tablets** have a floating **New email** button on the mail list.
