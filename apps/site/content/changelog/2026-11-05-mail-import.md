---
title: Move your mail and contacts here
date: 2026-11-05
summary: Copy an old mailbox's mail, folders and flags from another provider with an app password, keep new mail coming while you switch, import many mailboxes from a CSV file, and import contacts from vCard or CSV.
---

- **Import mail.** In the console, **Import mail** copies an old mailbox's mail into a mailbox here over IMAP, with an
  app password: folders (inside folders too), read, flagged and replied-to marks, labels and original dates. Choose
  Gmail, Yahoo Mail, iCloud Mail, AOL Mail or any other IMAP server. The old mailbox is only read, nothing is copied
  twice, and the app password is deleted when the import ends. Follow its progress, pause, resume or cancel it, and get
  a summary email when it finishes. See [Move your mail here](/docs/import).
- **Keep copying new mail while you switch.** Turn on **Keep copying new mail until I stop it** and new mail at the old
  provider keeps arriving here until you stop syncing, so moving a domain loses nothing. See
  [Switch a domain without losing mail](/docs/import/switching-a-domain).
- **Many mailboxes at once.** Owners and User Administrators can start an import for every mailbox in one CSV file,
  follow them on one page and download a report. See [Import many mailboxes](/docs/import/many-mailboxes).
- **Import contacts from CSV as well as vCard.** The webmail's **Import contacts** now reads CSV files too, with a step
  to check which column holds what, and merges people you already have instead of adding them twice. See
  [Import contacts](/docs/import/contacts).
- **Imported mail is labelled.** A copied message's details in the webmail say it was imported and not checked by us,
  and its original message view says the sender checks come from the old provider.
