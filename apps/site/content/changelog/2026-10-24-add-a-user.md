---
title: Add a user in one step
date: 2026-10-24
summary: Create a person and their mailbox together, with a role, a generated password and an optional first-sign-in password change.
---

- **Add a user** on the Mailboxes page creates the mailbox and the person's login in one go, in four steps: basics,
  role, optional profile details, and a review. No invite email: they sign in to the webmail and the console with
  their new address.
- **A password is created for you** and shown once, with Copy. You can type your own instead.
- **First sign-in password change:** on by default, the webmail and the console ask the new user to choose their own
  password before anything else.
- **The display name is the sender name** on mail they send from the webmail.
- **Cleaner sent mail:** messages from the webmail no longer carry an empty Cc header.
