---
title: Signatures
date: 2026-10-29
summary: Create several signatures with formatting and images, choose defaults for new mail and replies, and switch while you write.
---

- **Several signatures.** Under **Settings**, **Writing** in the webmail, **Add signature** opens an editor with the
  same formatting toolbar as your messages, and **Insert image** (or paste or drop a picture) adds a small image
  kept inside the signature. Links to images on other websites are removed, so a signature never tells another site
  when your mail is read. Each signature can be edited, deleted or duplicated.
- **Defaults.** Choose a signature for **new messages** and another for **replies and forwards**, or none for
  either. Signatures work with every address you send from.
- **Switch while you write.** The **Signature** button at the bottom of a message replaces the signature, and only
  the signature, with another one or removes it.
- Your existing signature is kept as **Signature**, the default for both. See
  [Signatures](/docs/folders-and-signatures#signatures).
