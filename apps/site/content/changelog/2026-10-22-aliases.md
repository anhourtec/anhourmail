---
title: Aliases and masked aliases
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Add more addresses to a mailbox, make random masked addresses for single sites, and switch either off.
---

- **Aliases:** add any address on your verified domains to a mailbox from the console's Aliases page.
- **Masked aliases:** one click makes a random address on the mailbox's domain, with a copy button and an optional
  note for where you used it.
- **On and off:** switch an alias off to refuse its mail while keeping the address, or delete it.
- Everyone in an account can manage the aliases of their own mailboxes; owners and user admins manage all of them.
- Role addresses (`postmaster@`, `abuse@`, `admin@`, `webmaster@` and similar) can only be added by owners and user
  admins.
