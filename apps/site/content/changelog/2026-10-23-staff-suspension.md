---
title: Account suspensions, explained
date: 2026-10-23
summary: When we have to suspend an account or a sign-in, you now get an email and a clear notice with the reason and how to appeal.
---

- **Clear notices:** if we suspend an account to stop spam or abuse, its Owners and administrators get an email with
  the reason, and the console shows a notice instead of its pages. A suspended sign-in is told so on the sign-in page.
- **Nothing is deleted:** mail, contacts, aliases and settings are kept while an account is suspended.
- **Appeals:** every notice says how to appeal, and a person reads every appeal.
- **Restored as it was:** when a suspension is lifted, the mailboxes it stopped come back; ones you had suspended
  yourself stay suspended.
- New docs page: [If your account is suspended](/docs/account-suspended).
