---
title: New alias limits for Free and paid plans
date: 2026-10-29
summary: Free accounts can have more aliases at a time and a yearly number of new ones; on paid plans the limit is per mailbox.
---

- **Free.** An account can have more aliases at a time than before, and create a set number of new aliases in a rolling
  year. Masked aliases keep their own limit at a time and get a yearly limit of their own. Deleting an alias
  frees a place but doesn't give back a new one for the year. **Aliases** in the console shows how many you have
  left this year, and the date you can add more once you reach the limit.
- **Paid plans.** Each mailbox can have a set number of addresses, aliases and masked aliases together, with no
  yearly limit. **Aliases** shows how many each mailbox has.
- The daily creation limit stays as it was. The numbers are in
  [How many can I have?](/docs/aliases#how-many-can-i-have).
