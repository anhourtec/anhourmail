---
title: Export your data and delete your account
date: 2026-10-29
summary: Download your mail and contacts from the webmail, export an account's data or your own from the console, and delete an account with a grace period you can cancel.
---

- **Mail and contacts.** In the webmail, **Settings → Export** downloads every folder as an mbox file and your
  contacts as a vCard file, in one ZIP made as it downloads. We never keep a copy.
- **Account and personal data.** Owners can export an account's settings, people, domains, mailboxes, aliases and
  audit log from **Account settings**; anyone can export their own details from **Your data**. We email you when it's
  ready, and you confirm it's you to download it.
- **Deleting an account.** An Owner types the account's name to delete it. Its mailboxes are suspended at once, and
  everything is removed after a grace period in which any Owner can cancel. Everyone in the account is emailed.
- **Deleting your sign-in.** From **Your data**, once no account depends on you.

See [Export your data and delete your account](/docs/export-and-deletion).
