---
title: A ceiling on addresses for very large accounts
date: 2026-11-04
summary: Very large accounts now have a ceiling on mailboxes and aliases together, which support can raise.
---

- **Address ceiling.** An account can hold a large but limited number of addresses: mailboxes, aliases and masked
  aliases together. Few accounts come near it. When one reaches it, creating another address says so; delete one you
  no longer use, or contact support to raise it. See [How many mailboxes](/docs/mailboxes#how-many-mailboxes).
