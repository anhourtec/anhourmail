---
title: Sign-in security
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app, recovery codes, passkeys, a Security page, and owners can require a second step.
---

- **Two-factor sign-in:** add a code from an authenticator app after your password. The set-up QR code is drawn in your
  browser, and each code works once.
- **Recovery codes:** saved once when you turn it on, each works once, and you're warned when few are left. Create a new
  set any time.
- **Passkeys and security keys:** sign in with your fingerprint, face, device PIN or a security key, with no password
  or code. Add, rename and remove them.
- **Security page:** see where you're signed in, sign out one session or everywhere else, and review recent sign-ins
  and changes. Open it from the menu under your initials.
- **Requirements for an account:** owners can require two-factor sign-in or a passkey for everyone in their account.
  People who don't have it yet are asked to set it up before they continue.
