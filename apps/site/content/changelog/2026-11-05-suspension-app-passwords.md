---
title: Suspensions for spam, abuse or phishing remove app passwords
date: 2026-11-05
summary: When we suspend a mailbox for spam, abuse or phishing, its app passwords are removed so mail apps signed in with them stop too.
---

- **App passwords go with an abuse suspension.** If we suspend an account or a person's sign-in for spam, abuse or
  phishing, the suspended mailboxes' app passwords are removed, which signs out mail apps that use them. After the
  suspension is lifted, make new ones on the App passwords page. Suspensions for other reasons leave them in place.
  See [If your account is suspended](/docs/account-suspended).
- **New mailboxes stay suspended too.** A mailbox added for someone whose sign-in is suspended starts suspended and
  comes back with their other mailboxes when the suspension is lifted.
