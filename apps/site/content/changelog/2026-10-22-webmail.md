---
title: Webmail, first look
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Read your mail in the browser — sign in with your mailbox address and password, with remote images blocked.
---

- **Sign in at the webmail address** with your mailbox address and mailbox password. Your password is used once and
  never stored; the session key stays on our servers, never in your browser.
- **Folders, conversations and a reading pane** side by side on a computer, one at a time on a phone. Unread
  counts, nested folders, flags, and keyboard shortcuts (`j`, `k`, `u`, `s`, `?`).
- **Remote images are blocked** in every message, and tracking pixels are removed, so senders can't tell when or
  where you opened their mail. Messages are cleaned on our servers and shown in an isolated frame.
- **Attachments download** straight to your device.
- **Open webmail** from your menu in the console, with your address already filled in.
- Writing mail in the webmail comes next.
