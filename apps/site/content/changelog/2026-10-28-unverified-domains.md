---
title: Unverified domains no longer hold a name for good
date: 2026-10-28
summary: Unfinished custom-domain setups are removed after a while, and a shared address made into a user loses its old app passwords.
---

- **Unverified domains expire.** A custom domain whose ownership and MX records were found has a limited time to be
  verified, and its records are checked daily; any other unverified domain is removed after a longer period. See
  [Connect your domain](/docs/custom-domain). Removed domains show in your audit log.
- **Someone else's unfinished setup no longer blocks you.** When your records are found but another account's setup
  of the same domain no longer has its verification record, theirs is removed and yours goes ahead. **Check now** tells
  you when another account still holds the domain.
- **Make this a user revokes old app passwords.** When a shared address becomes someone's mailbox, the app passwords
  made for it stop working, as its old password already did.
- **Profiles of people in more than one account** are edited only by the person themself.
