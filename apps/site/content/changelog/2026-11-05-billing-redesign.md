---
title: A clearer Billing page and a Usage tab
date: 2026-11-05
summary: Billing shows your plan and its price at a glance, plans as cards with what each includes, and a new Usage tab.
---

- **Billing, redesigned.** The plan card shows the plan, its price and when it renews, with cancelling and refunds at
  hand; next to it, what the plan includes and a quick look at usage. Plus and Supporter are cards listing what each
  includes, with a summary of the first year's total beside the add-ons. See [Plans and billing](/docs/billing).
- **Usage.** A new **Usage** tab on Billing shows mailboxes, storage, people and addresses against what the plan
  allows, each with a link to where you manage it.
- **Fixes.** The menu under your name now opens Billing (it said "Soon"), and it no longer shows table checkboxes
  through it.
