---
title: Billing in account deletion, exports and the webmail
date: 2026-11-05
summary: Deleting an account stops a paid plan renewing, account exports include the plan and billing state, and the webmail says when sending is paused for billing.
---

- **Deleting an account** stops a paid plan from renewing straight away and ends it before anything is removed, so
  nothing more is charged. Cancelling the deletion leaves the plan set to end: choose **Undo the cancellation** on
  Billing to keep it. See [Delete an account](/docs/export-and-deletion#delete-an-account).
- **Account exports** now include the account's plan and billing state: renewal or end date, add-ons and anything held
  on Free. Never card or payment details; invoices stay under **Manage payment and invoices**.
- **Webmail.** When sending is paused because of a payment problem, or a mailbox is held after a paid plan ended,
  **Send** says so and who can fix it. Reading and receiving keep working. Paid plans aren't open yet. See
  [Plans and billing](/docs/billing).
