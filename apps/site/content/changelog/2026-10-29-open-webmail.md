---
title: Open webmail from the console without typing your mailbox password
date: 2026-10-29
summary: Choosing Open webmail in the console now opens your own mailbox straight away, once, in the browser you started from.
---

- **One click.** **Open webmail** in the sidebar, your menu or on the home page opens your own mailbox in the webmail
  without asking for its password. With several mailboxes, you pick one.
- **Yours only.** It opens mailboxes assigned to you, never someone else's or a shared address, and never while
  Anhourtec support has your account open.
- **Can't be reused.** The link works once, for a short time, and only in the browser you started from. No password
  is stored or passed along. See [Open webmail from the console](/docs/webmail#open-webmail-from-the-console).
