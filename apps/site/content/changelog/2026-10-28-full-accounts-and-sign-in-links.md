---
title: Clearer full accounts and plainer sign-in links
date: 2026-10-28
summary: Add a user and new invites say why when an account is full, suspended accounts say so on Home, and webmail sign-in links no longer carry your address.
---

- **When an account is full**, the console says why instead of offering an action that would then be refused: all
  its people (counting pending invites), all the mailboxes its plan includes, or all its storage assigned. **Add a
  user** (in the navigation, on Home and on the Mailboxes page) and the navigation's new-invite button stay visible
  but greyed out, with the reason beside them. See [Mailboxes](/docs/mailboxes#add-a-user) and
  [People and roles](/docs/people-and-roles#invite-someone).
- **A suspended account** shows as suspended on the console's Home page, without the **Open webmail** link, since
  its mailboxes can't sign in. See [If your account is suspended](/docs/account-suspended).
- **Webmail sign-in links** from the console no longer fill in your address. A sign-in link carrying an email address
  looks like phishing to browsers, and anyone could craft one, so you type your address (or let your password
  manager fill it in). See [Webmail](/docs/webmail#sign-in).
