---
title: Confirm your email address when you sign up
date: 2026-11-06
summary: New accounts confirm their email address before the first sign-in.
---

- **Confirm at sign-up.** After you create an account we email you a link; open it to confirm your address, then sign
  in. Until then the console doesn't sign you in. If the link expired or didn't arrive, sign in with your email and
  password and we send a new one. See [Getting started](/docs).
- Getting your free address no longer asks you to confirm your email first: it's already done.
