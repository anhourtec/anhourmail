---
title: One composer with a fuller formatting toolbar
date: 2026-10-29
summary: The webmail composer always starts in rich text, with undo, fonts, sizes, colours, alignment and indent; plain text moves into More options.
---

- **More formatting.** The composer's toolbar now has undo and redo, font (sans serif, serif or monospace), text size,
  strikethrough, text colour and highlight, alignment, and decrease or increase indent, next to bold, italic,
  underline, lists, links and quotes. `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `K` adds a link. On a phone the toolbar scrolls sideways. See
  [Webmail](/docs/webmail#rich-text-and-html-email).
- **Plain text is in More options.** The Rich text / Plain text switch at the bottom of the composer is gone. To send
  text only, choose **More options** → **Plain text mode**; you're still asked before formatting is removed.
- **Tidier plain-text copies.** The plain-text copy sent with every rich message keeps one line per line you wrote,
  numbers numbered lists and doesn't mark indented text as a quote.
