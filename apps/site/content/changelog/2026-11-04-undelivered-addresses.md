---
title: Know when an address doesn't exist
date: 2026-11-04
summary: The webmail now tells you when an address you sent to can't get your message, and why.
---

- **Addresses that don't exist.** Sending to an address at AnhourMail that doesn't exist used to look like a normal
  send. Now a notice names the address and says why, for example "No mailbox with this address exists. (5.1.2)", and
  stays until you close it. Everyone else on the message still gets it, and your copy stays in **Sent**. This works
  for mail sent from a mailbox shared with you, too. See
  [If an address can't get your message](/docs/webmail#if-an-address-cant-get-your-message).
