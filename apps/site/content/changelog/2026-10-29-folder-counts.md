---
title: Folder counts stay up to date
date: 2026-10-29
summary: The counts beside your webmail folders update as soon as you read, move, delete, send or discard mail.
---

- **Folder counts stay current.** Discarding or sending a draft takes it off the Drafts count straight away, and
  reading, marking unread, moving, restoring, deleting, blocking a sender or reporting spam update the counts of
  every folder involved, without refreshing the page. Changes made in another mail app or on another device show up
  within half a minute. See [Webmail](/docs/webmail#reading-mail).
