---
title: Write mail in the webmail
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Compose, reply and forward with undo send, organise and search your mail, pin conversations, and switch between mailboxes.
---

- **Write, reply, reply all and forward** in the webmail, with address suggestions, attachments and your aliases as
  the sender. Drafts save themselves as you type.
- **Undo send:** a few seconds to change your mind after choosing Send.
- **Organise:** archive, delete, report junk, move to any folder, with Undo. Delete forever and empty Trash or Junk
  when you're sure.
- **Priority and pinned:** flagged mail collects under Priority; pinned conversations stay at the top of their
  folder.
- **Search** across your mail with `from:`, `to:`, `subject:`, `has:attachment` and `is:unread`, plus quick filters.
- **Select several** conversations at once (Shift for a range) and act on all of them.
- **Several mailboxes** in one webmail: add another mailbox and switch with one click.
- A refreshed layout: a collapsible sidebar, relative times, two-line previews, attachment chips with sizes, a reply
  box under every message, and a bottom action bar on phones. More keyboard shortcuts: `c`, `r`, `a`, `f`, `e`, `#`,
  `!`, `p`, `x` and `/`.
- **A better reader:** plain-text mail with working links, folded quotes and lighter signatures; formatted
  (markdown) messages; calendar invitations as event cards; forwarded messages that open in place; picture previews;
  View original and Print. The whole message scrolls as one page with the reply box at the bottom.
