---
title: Shared addresses, making them users, and editable profiles
date: 2026-10-28
summary: Shared addresses move into the Mailboxes menu, a shared address can become a user, and profile details can be edited later.
---

- **Add a user** is now the one main action on the Mailboxes page. Addresses that aren't one person's sign-in, like
  `support@`, are added from the **⋯** menu with **Add a shared address**.
- **Make this a user:** a mailbox that doesn't belong to anyone can become a person's own mailbox, with a role and
  optional profile details. A new password is created and shown once, and they choose their own at first sign-in.
- **Edit profile:** job title, department, office, phones and postal address can be changed after a user is added,
  by the person themself (new **Profile** page in the account menu) and by the account's admins.
