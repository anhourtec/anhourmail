- **All features in one place.** [All features](/docs/features) covers custom domains, aliases and masked aliases,
  privacy, the webmail, contacts, mail apps, families and small teams, sign-in security, the audit log and exporting
  your data, each with a link to its guide.
- **Honest about what's missing.** The same page lists what isn't built yet, such as paying for a plan and importing
  mail from another provider.
- **Clearer feature pages.** The site no longer mentions instant push or per-alias mail counts in the webmail, which
  aren't built yet, and the home page answers more questions: price, your own domain, masked aliases, and sharing an
  account with family or a team.
