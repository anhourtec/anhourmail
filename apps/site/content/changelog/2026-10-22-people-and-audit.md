---
title: People, roles and the audit log
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Invite people into your account with a role, change roles and remove people, and browse the full audit log.
---

- **Invite people:** on Plus and Supporter, invite family or a small group by email. The link works once, for that
  address only, and expires; resend or revoke it any time.
- **Roles:** owners give each person a role (Owner, Domain Administrator, User Administrator, Billing Administrator,
  Member or Read-only) and can change it later. User Administrators can invite and remove Members.
- **Safe by default:** an account always keeps at least one owner, nobody can change their own role, and only owners
  can manage owners and admins.
- **Audit log:** a filterable, paged list of everything that happened in your account, with details for each entry.
  Anything Anhourtec staff do is labelled as such.
