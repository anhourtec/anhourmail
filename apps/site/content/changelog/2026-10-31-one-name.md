---
title: One name, everywhere
date: 2026-10-31
summary: Your name in the console is the name on your mail; change it in either place and both show it.
---

- **One name.** Your name in the console is now also the sender name on mail from your mailboxes. Change it on
  **Profile** with **Edit name**, or in the webmail under **Settings**, **Account**, **Sender name**; the other place
  shows it when you come back to it, and mail you send from the webmail uses it straight away. Admins who can
  edit someone's profile can change their name on the **Profile** tab of their details panel. See
  [People and roles](/docs/people-and-roles#your-name).
- **Display names for mailboxes.** On **Mailboxes**, a person's mailbox uses their name; **Edit name**, then
  **Custom name**, gives it a name of its own (the webmail then shows it as set by your admin), and **Use the person's
  name** goes back. When someone leaves and their mailbox becomes a shared mailbox, it keeps the name it had.
- **Masked aliases stay anonymous.** Mail you send from a masked alias goes out with no sender name, only the address,
  so it can't be tied to you. Ordinary aliases send with your name. See
  [Aliases and masked aliases](/docs/aliases#masked-aliases).
