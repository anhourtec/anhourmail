---
title: Load remote images through our privacy proxy
date: 2026-10-29
summary: Load images now works in the webmail. Pictures come through our servers, so senders never see your IP address.
---

- **Load images.** Messages that link to pictures on other servers still open with those images blocked. Choose
  **Load images** to see them: our servers fetch them and show them in the message, so the sender sees our request,
  never your IP address, browser or cookies. Tracking pixels are still removed and never load. See
  [Remote images](/docs/webmail#remote-images-are-blocked-until-you-load-them).
- **Always load from this sender.** One click on the note makes a sender's images load by themselves from then on, and
  **Stop loading from this sender** undoes it.
- **A Remote images setting.** In **Settings → Reading** choose **Always ask**, **Load from contacts** or **Always
  load**, and forget every remembered sender at once. See
  [Webmail settings](/docs/webmail-settings#reading).
