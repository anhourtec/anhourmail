---
title: Removing someone turns off their mailbox sign-in
date: 2026-10-30
summary: When you remove someone from your account, their mailboxes stay as shared addresses and their sign-in to them is turned off at once.
---

- **Mail is kept.** Their mailboxes, mail and addresses stay, and mail keeps arriving. Each mailbox becomes a shared
  address that belongs to nobody.
- **Their way in is closed.** The mailbox gets a new password that nobody is shown, which signs them out of the
  webmail, and its app passwords are revoked, so their mail apps stop signing in. Before, someone who knew their
  mailbox password could still sign in after being removed.
- **You decide what's next.** Set a new password for whoever takes it over, make it someone's mailbox, or suspend or
  delete it. See [Change a role or remove someone](/docs/people-and-roles#change-a-role-or-remove-someone).
