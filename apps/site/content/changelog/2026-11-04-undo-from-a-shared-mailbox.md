---
title: Undo works from a shared mailbox
date: 2026-11-04
summary: Undo now stops a message you sent as or on behalf of a shared mailbox, as it does for your own.
---

- **Undo from a shared mailbox.** Choosing **Undo** right after sending as or on behalf of a mailbox shared with you
  could answer "It has already been sent." and let the message go. Now it stops it: nobody gets it, its copy leaves
  the shared mailbox's **Sent** folder, and your draft opens again, still from that mailbox. See
  [Sending as or on behalf of a mailbox](/docs/webmail#sending-as-or-on-behalf-of-a-mailbox).
