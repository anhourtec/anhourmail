---
title: A private automatic check at sign-up
date: 2026-11-05
summary: Signing up now runs a short automatic check in your browser that keeps automated sign-ups out, with no puzzles and no phone number.
---

- **An automatic check, nothing to solve.** While you fill in the sign-up form, your browser does a small calculation
  in the background, usually a second or two on a phone. The form shows its progress and says when it's done; there
  are no image puzzles and we don't ask for a phone number.
- **Nothing leaves for anyone else.** The check runs on your device and our servers only, with no outside service.
- **Fewer automated accounts.** We also limit how many accounts can be created from one network in a short time. If
  you see that message, try again later. See [Getting started](/docs#the-automatic-check-when-you-sign-up).
- Confirming your email address after signing up works as before.
