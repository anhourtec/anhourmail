---
title: Profile photos
date: 2026-10-29
summary: Add a profile photo in the console or the webmail; it shows everywhere you appear in your account.
---

- **Profile photos.** Upload a photo on **Profile** in the console or under **Settings**, **Account** in the webmail.
  It's one photo: it shows in the console's top bar and account menu, on **People and roles**, in the webmail
  sidebar and next to mail from you for people in your account, and a change in one place shows in the other when you
  come back to it. We crop it to a square and keep a small copy of our own without location or camera details. Admins
  who can edit someone's profile can change their photo too, and shared addresses can have their own. See
  [People and roles](/docs/people-and-roles#profile-photos).
- **Clearer names.** The webmail's name field is now called **Sender name**: it's the name people see on mail you
  send from that address, separate from your name in the console.
