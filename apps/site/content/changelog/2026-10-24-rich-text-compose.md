---
title: Write mail in rich text, or insert your own HTML
date: 2026-10-24
summary: The webmail composer now writes formatted mail, with a plain-text option and a safe way to insert HTML.
---

- **Rich text by default:** bold, italic, underline, links, lists, quotes and clear formatting from a small toolbar.
  Switch to **Plain text** at the bottom of the message any time.
- **A plain-text copy goes with every formatted message,** so mail apps that don't show formatting still show your
  words.
- **Safe pasting:** formatted text keeps its headings, lists, tables, links and colours; scripts, forms and the
  buttons some pages and extensions add are removed.
- **Insert HTML:** paste HTML or upload an .html file, preview exactly what will be sent, see what was removed, and get
  a warning when it loads images from another server.
- **Formatted replies and forwards** quote the original with its formatting.
