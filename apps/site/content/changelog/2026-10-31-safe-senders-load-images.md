---
title: Safe senders load their images
date: 2026-10-31
summary: One list of senders you trust — Safe senders now also load images, and mail you sent loads its images by itself.
---

- **One list.** Your **Safe senders** (Settings → Junk and blocked senders) now also load their remote images by
  themselves, through our privacy proxy, whichever **Remote images** setting you chose — but only when we can confirm
  the message really comes from them. A message that only pretends to be from a safe sender still asks first.
- **Always for** on a message's image note adds the sender's address to Safe senders, so their mail also stays out of
  Junk. **Stop loading from this sender** removes it again, after asking (and says so when a whole domain is on the
  list).
- **Your own mail.** Messages in your **Sent** folder load their images by themselves, through the same proxy.
- Senders you chose **Always for** before today still load. Settings → Reading shows how many there are, with
  **Forget them**. See
  [Loading images through our privacy proxy](/docs/webmail#loading-images-through-our-privacy-proxy).
