---
title: First-sign-in password change holds until it's applied
date: 2026-10-28
summary: A new user's webmail stays on the password step until their own password is in place.
---

- When you **Add a user** or **Make this a user** with a first-sign-in password change, their webmail now shows only
  the password step until their new password has actually been applied. If a change doesn't go through, the step says
  so and they can try again.
- Until they change it, the password you gave them also works in mail apps. The wizard now says so.
