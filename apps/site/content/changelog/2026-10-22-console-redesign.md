---
title: A new console layout
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Search across your account, a clearer navigation, details in a side panel and a home page that shows what to do next.
---

- **Search everything** from the bar at the top: mailboxes, aliases, domains and people you can see. Press `/` to
  start typing.
- **A new navigation** on the left, in the same style as the webmail: your accounts, one button for the thing you add
  most, counts beside each section, and help, keyboard shortcuts, theme and sign out at the bottom. It folds to icons
  (⌘B / Ctrl+B) and opens as a drawer on phones.
- **Every page works the same way:** actions above the list, a search box beside them, filters underneath, a ⋮ menu
  beside each name, and checkboxes to act on several mailboxes, aliases or people at once.
- **Details open in a panel** from the right, so the list stays in view. Each panel has its own address you can
  bookmark; old mailbox and domain links still work.
- **A home page that helps:** a greeting, quick actions, suggestions based on your account (a domain still waiting for
  DNS, a first mailbox, inviting people) and a summary of your mailboxes, people and domains.
