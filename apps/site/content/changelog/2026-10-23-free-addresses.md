---
title: Free addresses at our domain
date: 2026-10-23
summary: Every account can now get an address at our own domain. Pick a name, set a password, and start using it.
---

- **Get your free address** from the console's home page or the **Mailboxes** page: type a name, see straight away
  whether it's available, set the password you'll sign in with, and it's ready within a minute.
- **Confirm your email first.** We send a confirmation link when you sign up; you can ask for a new one from the
  console.
- **Names** are first come, first served (see the docs for the length and characters allowed). Very short names and names that could pass for us are
  reserved, and a name that was used once is never given to someone else.
- **Plans:** Free includes one address at our domain; on Plus and Supporter they count toward your mailboxes, next to
  the ones on your own domains.
- **Platform audit:** our Global Administrators now see the full IP address on platform events (sign-ups, password reset requests), so
  open sign-ups can be watched for abuse. Your account's audit log and your Security page still show only the network
  part.
