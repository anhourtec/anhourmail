---
title: Mailboxes on your own domain
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Create mailboxes on a verified domain, give them to people in your account and set their storage.
---

- **Create mailboxes** on any verified domain from the console's Mailboxes page, for someone in your account or for
  later.
- **Storage per mailbox** in whole GB, up to your plan's storage.
- A domain can't be removed while mailboxes use it.
