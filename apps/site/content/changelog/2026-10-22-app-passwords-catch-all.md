---
title: App passwords and catch-all
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Create a password for each mail app and revoke it when you lose the device; send mail for unknown addresses at your domain to one mailbox.
---

- **App passwords** for phone and desktop mail apps, on the new **App passwords** page. Name each one after its
  device, choose what it can do (read mail over IMAP or POP3, send over SMTP, mail over HTTPS with JMAP, contacts and calendars), and copy it:
  it's shown once and we never keep a copy.
- **Confirm it's you** before a new app password is made, and get an email each time one is created.
- **Revoke** one app password, or all of a mailbox's at once; the app stops signing in within a minute. People who
  manage mailboxes can see their names and revoke them, never the passwords.
- **Removing someone** from an account revokes the app passwords on their mailboxes.
- **Mail app settings** (servers, ports and security) sit next to your app passwords, ready to copy.
- **Catch-all** on your own domains (Plus and Supporter): mail to addresses that don't exist at the domain goes to a
  mailbox you choose. It's off until you turn it on, and turns itself off if that mailbox is suspended or deleted.
