---
title: Mail from other AnhourMail mailboxes counts as confirmed
date: 2026-10-30
summary: Remote images in mail from another AnhourMail mailbox, or from us, now offer Just this once or Always for the sender.
---

- **Fixed:** a message from another mailbox on AnhourMail, or one of our own messages to you, said we couldn't confirm
  it came from the sender's domain, so **Load images** couldn't remember the sender. Our servers now vouch for mail
  that the owner of the From address sent through them, so you get **Just this once** or **Always for** the sender.
- A message from outside that only pretends to be from an AnhourMail address, and a forwarded copy of one, still
  asks every time. See
  [Loading images through our privacy proxy](/docs/webmail#loading-images-through-our-privacy-proxy).
