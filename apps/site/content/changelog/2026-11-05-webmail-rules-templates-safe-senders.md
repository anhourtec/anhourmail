---
title: Mail rules, templates and safe senders for everyone
date: 2026-11-05
summary: Sort new mail with rules, insert text you write often from templates, and let your account's admins add safe senders for everyone.
---

- **Rules.** Webmail **Settings → Rules** sorts new mail as it arrives: match on who it's from or to, the subject, a
  mailing list, attachments or size, then move it to a folder or Trash, mark it read, flag it, or forward a copy to your
  confirmed forwarding address. **Check Inbox** shows what a rule would match before you save it. Rules run on our mail
  server, so they work in every mail app. See [Rules](/docs/webmail-settings#rules).
- **Templates.** Save text you write often in **Settings → Writing → Templates** and insert it from the **Templates**
  button while you write. See [Writing](/docs/webmail-settings#writing).
- **Safe senders for everyone.** Owners and User Administrators can add addresses and domains the whole account
  trusts, under **Mail → Safe senders** in the console: their images load (when we can confirm who sent the message)
  and the trusted-only Junk setting lets them through. Everyone sees them in their own settings, and anyone a person
  blocks stays blocked. See [Safe senders for everyone](/docs/safe-senders).
