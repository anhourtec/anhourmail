---
title: New mail arrives instantly in the webmail
date: 2026-11-04
summary: The webmail now shows new mail and changes from your other devices within a second or two, instead of checking every half minute.
---

- **New mail straight away.** While the webmail is open, new mail appears in the list as soon as it arrives, usually
  within a second or two; mail you read, move or delete in another app or on another device shows the same way. If
  that connection drops, the webmail checks every half minute until it's back. See
  [Reading mail](/docs/webmail#reading-mail).
- **Tabs stay in step.** Several tabs of the same mailbox share one connection for these updates, so reading a message
  in one tab shows it read in the others.
- Mailboxes shared with you still check every half minute.
