---
title: Anhourtec support access is read-only, short and in your audit log
date: 2026-10-29
summary: When Anhourtec support needs to see your console to help you, they open it read-only for a short time with a reason, your Owners are emailed, and every step is in the audit log.
---

- **Read-only.** A support person can see your settings, people, domains and mailboxes the way your admins do, but
  can't change anything, open webmail or read mail. Billing, deleting the account, roles, passwords and two-factor
  sign-in stay yours.
- **Short and explained.** It ends on its own after a short time (the guide has the limits), can only be extended with
  a new reason, and needs them to confirm it's them first.
- **Visible.** Your Owners get an email when it starts, with the reason. The audit log shows when it started and
  ended and everything they tried, as **Anhourtec staff · Support access**. See
  [Anhourtec support access](/docs/audit-log#anhourtec-support-access).
