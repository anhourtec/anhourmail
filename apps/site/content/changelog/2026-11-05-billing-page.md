---
title: The Billing page and invite codes
date: 2026-11-05
summary: See your plan and what the account uses on Billing, redeem invite codes, and read how billing will work once paid plans open.
---

- **Billing.** Owners and Billing Administrators have a **Billing** page in the console: the account's plan, what it
  uses (mailboxes, people, storage and addresses), and, once paid plans open, choosing Plus or Supporter, adding
  storage and extra mailboxes (you see and confirm the amount before anything is charged), cancelling, the one
  self-service refund and the payment portal for your card and invoices. Paid plans aren't open yet. See
  [Plans and billing](/docs/billing).
- **Payment notices.** If a payment fails, the people who look after billing see a notice at the top of the console;
  if sending has to pause, everyone in the account sees it, with who can fix it. Nothing is ever deleted because a
  payment failed: what doesn't fit the free plan is held, and **Choose what stays on** picks what stays on.
- **Invite codes.** A code from Anhourtec can let you sign up while sign-up is closed, give an account a paid plan for
  a while with no card, or both. Use it from the sign-up link, **Have an invite code?** when signing up, or
  **Redeem a code** on Billing. See [Invite codes](/docs/billing#invite-codes).
