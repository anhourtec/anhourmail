---
title: Webmail settings
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Make the webmail yours — layout and density, signatures, automatic replies, confirmed forwarding, blocked and safe senders, and more.
---

- **Settings in the webmail:** choose **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar or press `,`. Search finds any
  setting by what it does. Your choices are saved for your mailbox.
- **Appearance that changes as you click:** theme, compact / comfortable / roomy density, the reading pane on the
  right, at the bottom or off, preview lines, grouping by conversation, sender or subject first, initials, attachment
  names and date headers in the list.
- **Reading:** mark as read when opened, after a moment or never; what opens after you delete or archive; mark as
  read when moving.
- **Writing:** a signature for each of your addresses, Reply or Reply all as the default, an undo-send delay of 5 to
  30 seconds, and Cc and Bcc always shown if you like.
- **Automatic replies** for when you're away, with optional dates and a contacts-only option.
- **Forwarding** to one other address, after its owner confirms by email, with or without keeping a copy.
- **Blocked and safe senders**, and an option to only trust your contacts and safe senders. These run on the mail
  server, so they work in every app.
- **Account and region:** your name, aliases, storage, emptying Trash when you sign out, date and time formats, time
  zone, the folder to start in, and keyboard shortcuts on or off.
