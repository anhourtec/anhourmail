---
title: Storage is shared by the whole account
date: 2026-10-23
summary: Plus and Supporter include 10 GB shared by everyone in the account for $9 a year per account; you choose each mailbox's share.
---

- **One price per account:** Plus is $9 USD / $12 CAD a year per account and covers everyone in it, with ten mailboxes
  included. Free keeps 1 GB for its one mailbox.
- **10 GB shared by the whole account** on Plus and Supporter. Each mailbox gets a share, starting at an even share of
  what's unassigned, and you can move storage between mailboxes from their Storage tab. The Mailboxes page shows how
  much the account uses, how much is assigned and how much is left. A mailbox's share can't go below the mail it holds,
  and nothing is ever deleted.
- **Coming with billing:** more storage, +10 GB for $10 a year, and extra mailboxes at $1 a year each. This replaces the
  earlier fair-use line. Nothing is charged automatically.
