---
title: Archive works in every mailbox
date: 2026-10-29
summary: Every mailbox now has an Archive folder, so the Archive button and the e key move mail out of your inbox.
---

- **Archive works.** Mailboxes didn't get an Archive folder, so the Archive button and the `e` key did nothing. Every
  mailbox now has one, new and existing alike, and archiving moves a conversation out of the Inbox into Archive
  without deleting it. Mail apps that use IMAP see it as their archive folder. If a mailbox ever has no Archive, the
  webmail now says nothing was moved instead of doing nothing. See
  [Folders and signatures](/docs/folders-and-signatures#folders).
