---
title: Contacts in the webmail
date: 2026-10-22
summary: Keep your contacts in the webmail, with people you email saved automatically and syncing to your phone and computer.
---

- **Contacts** in the webmail sidebar: an A to Z list with search, labels, **My contacts** and **Other contacts**,
  and each person's addresses, phones, company, birthday and notes. Add, edit and delete them, or several at once.
- **People you email are saved automatically** to Other contacts, so their addresses come up when you write. Nobody
  already in your contacts is changed, and you can turn it off.
- **Suggestions while you write** now include your contacts, with the people you email most first.
- **Add to contacts** from a message's sender or an address you're writing to.
- **Import and export** vCard (`.vcf`) files.
- **Sync to your phone and computer** over CardDAV, signed in with an app password.
- Buttons now show their name and keyboard shortcut as soon as you pause over them.
