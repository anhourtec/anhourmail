---
title: A clearer authenticator set-up
date: 2026-11-06
summary: Turning on an authenticator app now walks you through three numbered steps.
---

- **Authenticator app set-up** on your Security page now shows three steps — open your app, scan the code, type the
  code it shows — with the code in its own card. Any authenticator app that shows 6-digit codes works. See
  [Sign-in security](/docs/sign-in-security).
