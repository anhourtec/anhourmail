---
title: Permanent email addresses only
date: 2026-11-06
summary: Temporary, disposable inboxes can't be used to sign up, to invite someone or as a forwarding address.
---

- **Sign-up, invites and forwarding need a permanent address.** Addresses from temporary, disposable inbox services
  are refused when you sign up, when someone invites a person into their account, and when you set a forwarding
  address in the webmail. The address you sign up with is where sign-in links, billing notices and account alerts go,
  so it needs to be one you'll keep. If a real address of yours is refused, contact support and we'll look at it. See
  [Getting started](/docs).
