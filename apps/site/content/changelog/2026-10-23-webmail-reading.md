---
title: Conversations, Show original and automatic refresh in the webmail
date: 2026-10-23
summary: Whole conversations in the reader, a complete message menu, a Show original page, Restore from Trash, and new mail without pressing Refresh.
---

- **Conversations:** every message of a conversation is shown oldest first, earlier ones folded to a line. The newest
  and any unread messages open expanded; Reply, Reply all and Forward sit at the bottom.
- **Message menu:** reply, delete, mark unread from here, block the sender, report spam or phishing, print, download
  the message and Show original.
- **Show original** opens in a new tab with the message ID, delivery time, SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, and the full
  source as text, with Download original and Copy to clipboard.
- **Restore** in Trash puts a message back in your Inbox (your own messages go back to Sent); **Not spam** in Junk
  moves it to the Inbox.
- **New mail appears on its own** while the webmail is open, with an optional chime and desktop notification
  (Settings → Reading).
