---
title: Free mailboxes get 1 GB; paid mailboxes 10 GB
date: 2026-10-23
summary: See how much storage each mailbox uses, new plan prices, and storage per mailbox set to 1 GB on Free and 10 GB on Plus and Supporter.
---

- **Storage used, in the console.** The Mailboxes list shows how much each mailbox holds out of its quota, with a bar
  that turns amber when it's getting full and red when it's almost full (with the words, not just the colour). Sort
  by the Storage column to find the biggest. The mailbox's Storage tab shows the amount, the percentage and when it
  was last measured, and the home page shows your account's total. Storage is measured every ten minutes or so; only
  sizes are read, never anything about your messages.
- **Storage per mailbox:** 1 GB on Free, 10 GB on Plus and Supporter. Existing mailboxes were brought down to the new
  amount, except where they already held more: those keep enough room for their mail, so nothing stops arriving and
  nothing is deleted.
- **New prices:** Plus is now $9 USD / $12 CAD a year with ten mailboxes included, and Supporter starts at $20 USD /
  $27 CAD a year.
- **No hard limits on paid accounts:** fair use of 30 GB stored per account, then $1 per GB a year; extra mailboxes
  $1 a year each. Nothing is charged for either until billing opens, and we'll tell you before anything is.
