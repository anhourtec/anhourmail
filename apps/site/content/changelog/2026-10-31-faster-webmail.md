---
title: The webmail opens faster
date: 2026-10-31
summary: Your inbox arrives with the page instead of one step after another, and the webmail can show your last first page at once while it loads.
---

- **Faster to your inbox.** Your folders, their counts and the first screen of your Inbox now arrive together with
  the webmail page itself, instead of one step after another once it has loaded. On a slow or distant connection the
  inbox appears in about half the time.
- **Shown at once on your next visit.** The webmail keeps a copy of the first page you saw on this device — what the
  list shows, never a whole message or an attachment's content, encrypted so it can't be read once you're signed out — and shows it the moment you open the webmail, then replaces it with what's on
  our servers. Signing out, switching mailbox or the webmail finding your session has ended removes it. See
  [Webmail](/docs/webmail#what-the-webmail-keeps-on-your-device).
