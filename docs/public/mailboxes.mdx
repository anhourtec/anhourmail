---
title: Mailboxes
description: Create mailboxes on your own domain, give them to people in your account, set their storage, and suspend, delete or restore them.
---

A mailbox is an address with its own storage, like `jane@example.org`. On the Plus and Supporter plans you can
create mailboxes on any domain you've connected and verified (see [Use your own domain](/docs/custom-domain)).

## Create a mailbox

1. In the console, open **Mailboxes**.
2. Type the part before the @ and choose the domain. Letters, numbers, dots, hyphens and underscores work;
   `+` is reserved, because `you+anything@` already reaches you.
3. Choose who it **belongs to**, or leave it for later.
4. Set its **storage** in whole GB, up to your plan's storage per mailbox.

The mailbox shows **Setting up** for a moment, then **Active**.

## Change storage

Open the mailbox and change **Storage**. The new quota applies within a minute. Lowering it below what the mailbox
already holds doesn't delete anything, but the mailbox stops accepting new mail until there's room again.

## Suspend a mailbox

Open the mailbox and choose **Suspend mailbox**. Within a minute:

- Nobody can sign in to it: webmail, mail apps (IMAP, POP3 and SMTP) and app passwords are all refused. A mail app
  that is already connected can stay connected until it disconnects, but receives no new mail.
- Mail sent to it or to its aliases isn't delivered; the sender gets a bounce.
- Nothing in it is deleted. Messages, folders, settings and app passwords are all kept.

Choose **Resume mailbox** to turn it back on. It shows **Setting up** for a moment, then **Active**.

## Delete a mailbox

Open the mailbox, choose **Delete mailbox…**, and confirm. A deleted mailbox can be restored for

<Value of="mailboxDeleteGraceDays" /> days:

- Straight away it's suspended (see above), and its aliases stop receiving mail too.
- Its address, and its aliases' addresses, stay reserved, so nobody else can take them.
- The list shows it as **Deleting**, with the date until which you can restore it.

After that date the mailbox, its aliases and all of its mail are permanently deleted, and the addresses are free
again. This can't be undone.

### Restore a deleted mailbox

Before that date, open the mailbox and choose **Restore mailbox**. It comes back with its aliases and all of its mail,
and shows **Active** again within a minute (even if it was suspended before you deleted it). A mailbox can't be
restored if its domain is no longer verified in your account.

## Who can manage mailboxes

Owners and User Admins can create, suspend, delete and restore mailboxes and change their storage. Only an owner can
suspend, delete or restore a mailbox that belongs to an owner or an admin. Domain Admins and Auditors can see
mailboxes.

If a suspension or deletion couldn't be applied on the mail server, the mailbox shows **Not applied** and its page
offers **Retry**.

A domain can't be removed while mailboxes use it, including deleted mailboxes that can still be restored.
