AnhourMail is for personal mail. Limits on sending keep spammers off the service, which keeps everyone's mail
out of spam folders. Receiving mail is never limited by these levels.

## Newsletters and bulk mail

The standard plans are for personal mail, so newsletters, bulk mail and marketing mail aren't allowed on them.

accounts. It sends from a separate pool, only to people who opted in, with one-click unsubscribe — Canada's anti-spam
law (CASL) applies. The [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) has the details.

## If sending is paused

If an account sends something unusual, sending may pause while we take a look. Receiving and reading mail keep
working. You'll get an email explaining what happened and what to do next.
