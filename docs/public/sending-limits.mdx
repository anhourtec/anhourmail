AnhourMail is for personal mail. Limits on sending keep spammers off the service, which keeps everyone's mail
out of spam folders. Receiving mail is never limited by these levels.

## See your level

- **In the console:** the home page shows a **Sending** card for each of your accounts, with its level and limits.
  Owners and admins see it; members see their own mailbox's level in the webmail.
- **In the webmail:** **Settings → Account → Sending** shows the level of the mailbox you're in, its limits, and what
  that means for its forwarding and automatic replies.

Neither shows when a level will change: it changes on its own once the account is established, or straight away when
you pay for a plan.

## New accounts

A new account can send to addresses hosted on AnhourMail (free addresses and customers' own domains) from the first
minute. Sending elsewhere, and sending from mail apps over SMTP, opens automatically once your account is established;
paying for a plan opens it straight away. A plan you didn't pay for (a free trial, a code or a gift) waits like a free
account. A refund or a dispute takes it back, as does the plan ending within <Value of="refundWindowDays" /> days
of the payment that opened it. Until then:

- the webmail tells you before anything is sent: "New accounts can send to addresses hosted on AnhourMail for now",
  and lists the recipients that may be outside AnhourMail. Remove them and send again: the rest goes out at once, and
  your draft is kept until then;
- a mail app that sends over JMAP gets each outside recipient back as not delivered;
- mail apps read your mail over IMAP and POP3 as usual, but signing in to send over SMTP is refused.

Receiving mail from anyone works from the start. Automatic replies, and forwarding to an address outside AnhourMail,
are paused until your account is established: you can set them up now, and they start on their own then. **App
passwords** in the console says that mail apps can't send yet, for each mailbox.

## When the webmail can't send

| You see                                                           | What it means                                                 | What to do                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| "New accounts can send to addresses hosted on AnhourMail for now" | The account is new and a recipient is outside AnhourMail.     | Remove the outside recipients, or wait.               |
| "You've reached your sending limit for now"                       | Your level's limit per hour or per day is used up.            | Try again later. Your draft is kept.                  |
| "Sending is paused for this mailbox"                              | Sending is paused while we take a look (see below).           | Write to the support address shown to ask us to look. |
| "Sending isn't available right now"                               | We couldn't check your limits just then, so nothing was sent. | Try again in a moment. Your draft is kept.            |

## Newsletters and bulk mail

The standard plans are for personal mail, so newsletters, bulk mail and marketing mail aren't allowed on them.

<Value of="bulkAddOnName" /> will be a paid add-on (<Value of="bulkAddOnStatus" />) for <Value of="bulkAddOnPlans" />
accounts. It sends from a separate pool, only to people who opted in, with one-click unsubscribe, following anti-spam
law. The [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) has the details.

## If sending is paused

If an account sends something unusual, sending may pause while we take a look, for the whole account or for one
mailbox. Receiving and reading mail keep working; forwarding and automatic replies pause too. The console's
**Sending** card says why, and where to write to ask us to look again. You'll get an email explaining what happened
and what to do next.
