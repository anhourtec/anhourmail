title: Contacts

Your contacts live with your mailbox, on our mail server, in standard address books. The webmail shows them, and your
phone and computer can sync them too (see [Sync with your phone and computer](#sync-with-your-phone-and-computer)).
Each mailbox has its own contacts.

## Open your contacts

Choose **Contacts** in the webmail sidebar, under your folders. On a phone, open the sidebar with the button at the
top left first.

- Contacts are listed A to Z. Type in **Search contacts** to find someone by name, address, company or phone number.
- The buttons above the list show **All**, **My contacts**, **Other contacts**, or the people with one label.
- Choose a contact to see their addresses, phone numbers, company, birthday, labels and notes. Choose an address
  (or **Send email**) to write to them. On a phone the contact replaces the list; the back arrow returns to it.

## Add and edit contacts

1. Choose the **New contact** button (a person with a plus) above the list.
2. Fill in what you know: first and last name, company, up to ten email addresses and phone numbers, a birthday (the
   year is optional), labels and notes. A contact needs at least a name, an address or a phone number. Each mailbox can keep up to 5,000 contacts.
3. Choose **Save**.

To change a contact, open it and choose the pencil. To delete it, choose the bin and confirm — this removes it from
every device that syncs your contacts, and can't be undone.

You can also add someone straight from your mail: **Add to contacts** appears beside a sender's address when you
read their message, and the small person button on an address you're writing to adds that address.

**Labels** group people (for example `family` or `book club`). Type them separated by commas; each label gets its own
button above the list.

## People you email are saved automatically

When you send a message, anyone you wrote to who isn't in your contacts yet is added to **Other contacts**, with the
name the message used, a few seconds later once the message has really gone (if you choose **Undo**, nobody is
saved). Nobody already in your contacts is changed. This keeps their addresses in the suggestions
when you write, without filling **My contacts**.

- To keep someone, open them and choose **Move to My contacts** in the ⋯ menu.
- To remove someone, delete them as usual.
- To stop saving people automatically, open the ⋯ menu above the contact list and turn off
  **Automatically save people I email**. It's on unless you turn it off, and the setting is per mailbox.

## Select several

Pause over the initials beside a contact and tick the box (or tick several). The bar that appears can move them
to My contacts or Other contacts, export them, or delete them (after a confirmation).

## Import and export

- **Import:** in the ⋯ menu above the list, choose **Import contacts (.vcf)** and pick a vCard file exported from
  another app or service. Files can be up to 1 MB and 1,000 contacts. We read names, email addresses, phone
  numbers, company, birthday, labels and notes; photos and links in the file are ignored. Contacts without a name,
  address or phone number are skipped, and the notice tells you how many.
- **Export:** choose **Export all contacts** in the same menu, or select some contacts and choose the download
  button, to get a `.vcf` file you can import anywhere.

## Sync with your phone and computer

Your contacts use CardDAV, the standard most phones and desktop apps understand, so changes on one device show up on
the others.

You'll need:

| Setting  | Value                                                                     |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Server   | <Value of="carddavHost" />                                                |
| Username | Your full email address                                                   |
| Password | An app password (create one in your account settings, as for mail apps)   |
| URL      | <Value of="carddavUrl" /> followed by your email address, if the app asks |

- **iPhone and iPad:** Settings → Contacts → Accounts → Add Account → Other → **Add CardDAV Account**. Enter the
  server, your email address and the app password.
- **Mac:** Contacts → Settings → Accounts → **+** → Other Contacts Account → CardDAV, with the same values.
- **Android:** Android needs a free CardDAV sync app (for example DAVx⁵ from F-Droid or Google Play). Add an account
  with "Login with URL and user name", using the server above as the URL.
- **Thunderbird:** Address Book → New Address Book → **CardDAV Address Book**, and enter your email address and the
  server; Thunderbird finds the address books itself.

Your address books show up as your mailbox's address book (shown as **My contacts** in the webmail) and **Other
contacts**.

<Callout title="Use an app password">
  Like mail apps, contact apps sign in with an app password, never your account password. If you lose a device, revoke
  its app password and nothing else changes.
</Callout>
