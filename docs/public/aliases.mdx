## Aliases

An alias is another address that delivers to the same inbox. Use one for work enquiries, another for family, and
reply from whichever address the mail came to.

## Masked aliases

A masked alias is a random address you give to a shop, a newsletter or a sign-up form. If it starts getting spam,
switch it off and the mail stops. Each masked alias shows how much mail it receives, so you can see who shares
your address.

## Catch-all

On paid plans with your own domain, a catch-all delivers mail sent to any address at the domain that doesn't
exist yet. It is off by default because it also catches spam sent to made-up names.
