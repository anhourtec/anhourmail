---
title: Webmail settings
description: Make the webmail look and behave the way you like — theme, density and reading pane, signatures, automatic replies, forwarding, blocked and safe senders, your photo and sender name, dates and keyboard shortcuts.
---

Open settings with **Settings** at the bottom of the webmail sidebar, or press `,` (comma). On a phone, open the
sidebar with the button at the top left first; settings then fill the screen.

Type in **Search settings** to find a setting by name or by what it does — "vacation", "signature", "dark",
"forward" and so on. Settings are saved for your mailbox, so they follow you to any browser where you sign in to it.

Most settings take effect as soon as you change them and the mail behind the window updates straight away.
Automatic replies, forwarding, blocked senders and your sender name have their own **Save** button (signatures save from their own editor),
because they change how your mail is handled. Your photo is saved as soon as you choose it.

## Appearance

- **Theme:** follow your device, or always light or dark. The switch at the bottom of the sidebar changes the same
  setting.
- **Density:** compact, comfortable or roomy text and spacing in the message list. The preview at the top of the
  panel shows the result.
- **Reading pane:** messages open on the right of the list, below it, or — with **Off** — in place of the list.
- **Motion:** follow your device, or always reduce animations.
- **Group messages:** one row per conversation, or every message on its own row.
- **Message preview:** none, one line or two lines of each message under its subject.
- **Sender first:** show the sender on the first line of a row; turn it off to show the subject first.
- **Sender initials**, **attachment names** under each message, and **date headers** (Today, Yesterday, Last week…)
  between messages.

## Reading

- **Mark as read:** when you open a message, after a few seconds, or never (mark it yourself with `Shift`+`I`).
- **After deleting or archiving:** open the next message, the previous one, or go back to the list.
- **Mark as read when moving:** archiving, reporting junk or moving to a folder also marks the message read.
- **Play a sound for new mail** while the webmail is open.
- **Desktop notifications** show who sent new Inbox mail (never what it says). Your browser asks for permission the
  first time. If it blocked notifications for this site, the setting explains how to allow them again: open the site
  settings (the icon at the left of the address bar), set **Notifications** to **Allow**, then turn the setting on.
- **Remote images:** **Always ask** (the default: images on other servers stay blocked until you choose **Load
  images**), **Load from contacts** (images load by themselves when the sender is in your contacts) or **Always
  load**. Images always come through our privacy proxy, so senders never see your IP address, and tracking pixels are
  always removed. See
  [Remote images are blocked until you load them](/docs/webmail#remote-images-are-blocked-until-you-load-them).
- **Safe senders:** images from your Safe senders always load (when we can confirm the message comes from them).
  **Manage them in Junk and blocked senders** takes you there.
- **Senders saved earlier:** shows only if you chose **Always for** a sender before Safe senders loaded images: how
  many there are (only a private fingerprint of each address was kept, so they can't be listed). Their images still
  load; **Forget them** clears them.
- Read receipts are never sent.

## Writing

- **Signatures:** **Add signature** to create one with formatting and small embedded images; the list has **Edit**,
  **Delete** and **More options → Duplicate** for each. **Default for new messages** and **Default for replies and
  forwards** choose the signature each kind of message starts with (or **(No signature)**), and the **Signature**
  button in a message lets you switch while you write. Signatures work with every address you send from. Mail apps
  on your phone or computer keep their own signatures. See [Signatures](/docs/folders-and-signatures#signatures).
- **Reply button:** whether **Reply** and the `r` key reply to the sender or to everyone.
- **Undo send:** how long you have to take a message back after choosing **Send** — 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds.
- **Always show Cc and Bcc** in new messages.

## Automatic replies

Turn on **Send automatic replies** to answer mail while you're away. You can:

- limit them to **between these dates** (in your time zone, both days included);
- give them a subject, and write the message people get;
- **only reply to my contacts**, so strangers and mailing lists don't hear from you.

Each sender gets one automatic reply every few days, however often they write. Mail from blocked senders, and mail
your rules send to Junk, gets no reply. Automatic replies work for mail to any of your addresses, whichever app you
read it in.

## Forwarding

Send new mail to one other address:

1. Enter the address under **Forward to** and choose **Send confirmation**.
2. We email that address a link. Its owner opens it and chooses **Confirm forwarding**. The link works once, for
   three days. Until it's confirmed, nothing is forwarded.
3. Back in settings, turn on **Forward my mail**. Choose whether to **keep a copy here** too.

You can't forward to one of your own addresses, and you can't set up forwarding while sending from your mailbox is
paused (see [sending limits](/docs/sending-limits)). Mail from blocked senders and mail your rules send to Junk is
never forwarded. Mail that fails its sender's domain checks isn't forwarded either, and neither is mail from outside
that claims to come from your own domain unless we can confirm it did; it stays in your inbox. **Remove address** stops forwarding and
forgets the address; to forward somewhere else, remove it and start again.

## Junk and blocked senders

- **Blocked senders:** mail from these addresses or whole domains (type `example.com`) goes straight to Junk.
- **Safe senders:** mail from these addresses or domains is never moved to Junk, and their images load (only when we
  can confirm the message really comes from them). **Always for** on a message's image note adds a sender here.
  Domains match exactly: `example.com` doesn't include `news.example.com`. Each list holds up to
  <Value of="senderListEntries" /> entries.
- **Only trust my contacts and safe senders:** everything else goes to Junk. Check your Junk folder now and then if
  you turn this on.

To deal with one message, use **Report spam** (`!`) or **Not spam** on it instead. Your automatic replies,
forwarding and junk rules run on our mail server, so they also apply to mail you read in other apps. These rules are
managed here, in your account's control panel and the webmail — a filter editor for your own rules is a later
feature.

## Account

- **Photo:** your profile photo, the same one as in the console, shown in the webmail sidebar and next to mail from
  you for people in your account. **Upload photo** takes a PNG, JPEG or WebP up to <Value of="photoMaxUpload" />,
  cropped to a square; **Remove** goes back to your initials. If you belong to more than one account, change it on
  your console **Profile** instead (the webmail links there). For a shared mailbox with no person behind it, this is
  the mailbox's own photo. More in [People and roles](/docs/people-and-roles#profile-photos).
- **Sender name**, as people see it on mail you send from this mailbox and its aliases. It's your name, the same one
  your console profile shows: changing it here changes it there, and the other way round
  ([your name](/docs/people-and-roles#your-name)). If an admin gave this mailbox its own display name, or it's a
  shared mailbox, it's shown here as set by your admin and you change it in the console. If you belong to more than one
  account, change it on your console **Profile**. Mail from a [masked alias](/docs/aliases#masked-aliases) goes out
  with no name, only the address, so it can't be tied to you. Names can't contain `@`, web addresses, `=?` or
  control characters.
- **Addresses you can send from:** your aliases. Manage them in the console.
- **Storage:** how much of your mailbox's space you use.
- **Password:** change your mailbox password. Enter your current password and a new one (or choose **Generate a
  strong password** and save it in your password manager). The new password needs 12 to 128 characters, no spaces at
  the start or end, can't be your address or start with `app_`, and can't be one that has appeared in a known data
  breach ([how the check works](/docs/sign-in-security#passwords-seen-in-data-breaches)). You stay signed in here; the webmail signs out everywhere
  else. App passwords for your mail apps keep working. If you forgot your password, ask an owner or admin of your
  account to set a new one. Right after an admin sets a new password for you, you can't change it here with the
  previous one: sign in with the new password once it works (within a minute), or ask them to set it again.
- **Empty Trash when I sign out** deletes everything in Trash, for good, each time you sign out of the webmail.
  Otherwise Trash is cleaned up automatically after a while.
- **Sign out of this mailbox.**

## Region and shortcuts

- **Region:** how dates look (English for now). **Time format:** the region's, 12-hour or 24-hour.
- **Time zone:** this device's, or one you choose. Automatic reply dates use it too.
- **Start in:** the folder the webmail opens with — Inbox, Priority or any folder.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** on or off, with the full list. Turn them off if they get in the way of a screen reader's
  own keys.
