The webmail is AnhourMail in your browser, at <Value of="webmailHost" />. It's a separate address from the console
(<Value of="appHost" />), so signing in to one doesn't sign you in to the other.

## Sign in

1. Go to <Value of="webmailHost" />. From the console you can also open your menu and choose **Open webmail**,
   which fills in your address for you.
2. Enter your **mailbox address** and your **mailbox password** — the password of the mailbox itself, not an app
   password.
3. Choose **Sign in**.

If the address or password is wrong you'll see "Mailbox address or password is wrong." We don't say which one, so nobody
can use the form to find out which addresses exist. After several wrong tries in a row, wait a few minutes before
trying again.

Your password is only used to sign in. We don't store it: the mail server checks it and gives your browser session
a key that stays on our servers, never in the browser. Your mailbox password is the one set when the mailbox was
created; change it in [Settings → Account](/docs/webmail-settings#account). Changing it keeps you signed in where you
changed it and signs the webmail out everywhere else; when an admin sets a new one, the webmail signs out everywhere.
Forgot it? Ask an owner or admin of your account to [set a new one](/docs/mailboxes#set-a-new-password).

If you see "This mailbox can't be used right now", the mailbox has been suspended or deleted by your account's admin
(see [Mailboxes](/docs/mailboxes)). Ask them to resume or restore it.

## Several mailboxes

If you use more than one mailbox, open the menu (⋮) at the top of the sidebar and choose **Add another mailbox**, then
sign in to it with its own address and password. Each mailbox gets a square tile at the top of the sidebar; choose a
tile to switch. You can have up to five open at once. **Sign out** signs out of all of them.

## Reading mail

- On a computer you see your folders, the list of conversations and the open message side by side. On a phone you
  see one at a time: choose a conversation to open it, and the back arrow to return to the list. The sidebar folds
  into a narrow rail with the button at the top of the list, and is a drawer on phones.
- The list shows the newest first, with how long ago each arrived ("24 minutes ago", "1 day ago", then the date). A
  blue dot after the sender means unread; the number beside a sender is how many messages the conversation holds.
- Opening a conversation marks it read. Use the arrows at the top of the message to go to the previous or next one.
- Plain-text messages keep their line breaks, and links and addresses in them work. Earlier messages quoted in a
  reply are folded away behind **…** — choose it to show them; signatures are shown lighter.
- Attachments are listed under the sender, with their size. Pictures show a small preview; choose one to see it
  bigger. Forwarded messages open in place. Everything else downloads when you choose it; files are never opened in
  the webmail itself.
- Calendar invitations show as a card with the time, place and organiser. Replying to invitations is coming soon.
- In the ⋮ menu, **Show original** shows the message's source, and **Print** opens a printable copy.
- Very long messages show the first part with **View entire message**.
- **Priority** in the sidebar collects every message you've flagged, in any folder.
- **Pinned** conversations stay at the top of their folder, in their own section. Use the pin button on a message.

## Writing mail

- Choose **New email** in the sidebar (or press `c`). Type addresses in **To**; press Enter or a comma after each
  one, or pick from the suggestions (your [contacts](/docs/contacts) and people you've written to, the ones you
  email most first). **Cc Bcc** adds those fields. People you send to are saved to **Other contacts** automatically
  (you can turn this off).
- If you have aliases or masked aliases, choose which address to send from in **From**.
- Attach files with the paperclip (up to <Value of="attachmentPerFile" /> each, <Value of="attachmentPerMessage" /> per
  message).
- Your message is saved to **Drafts** a couple of seconds after you stop typing. Close it and carry on later from the
  Drafts folder; the bin button discards it.
- To reply, use the **Reply to …** box under a message, or the reply button. **Reply all**, **Forward** and more are
  in the ⋮ menu. Replies quote the original message and stay in the same conversation.
- **Undo send:** after you choose **Send**, you have a few seconds to choose **Undo** — the message goes back to your
  drafts and nothing is delivered.
- You can send to up to <Value of="recipientsPerMessage" /> people per message, and each person counts toward your
  [sending limits](/docs/sending-limits). Writing is plain text for now.

## Organising mail

- **Archive** keeps a conversation but takes it out of the Inbox. **Delete** moves it to Trash; in Trash (and Junk)
  you can delete forever, after a confirmation. Both Trash and Junk have **Empty** in their ⋯ menu.
- **Report spam** moves a conversation to Junk and helps the filters learn; **Not spam** moves it back.
- **Move to** in the ⋮ menu files a conversation in any folder.
- Archive, delete, junk and move show a notice with **Undo** for a few seconds.
- **Select several:** hover over the square avatar and tick it (or press `x`); hold Shift to tick a range. The bar that
  appears acts on all of them.

## Search and filters

Type in **Search…** above the list (or press `/`) and press Enter. Search covers every folder except Trash and Junk
(search inside Trash or Junk while you are in them). You can narrow it with:

| Write             | To find                   |
| ----------------- | ------------------------- |
| `from:sam`        | messages from someone     |
| `to:jordan@…`     | messages to someone       |
| `subject:"cabin"` | words in the subject      |
| `has:attachment`  | messages with attachments |
| `is:unread`       | unread messages           |
| `is:flagged`      | messages in Priority      |

The filter button beside the title shows only unread, Priority, or messages with attachments.

### Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` in the webmail (or choose **Keyboard shortcuts** in the sidebar) to see them all. Pausing over a button
also shows its name and shortcut.

| Key                       | What it does                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| `j` / `k`                 | Next / previous conversation   |
| `Enter` or `o`            | Open the selected conversation |
| `u` or `Esc`              | Back to the list               |
| `c`                       | New email                      |
| `r` / `a` / `f`           | Reply / reply all / forward    |
| `e`                       | Archive                        |
| `#`                       | Delete                         |
| `!`                       | Report spam                    |
| `s`                       | Add to or remove from Priority |
| `p`                       | Pin or unpin                   |
| `x`                       | Select                         |
| `Shift`+`U` / `Shift`+`I` | Mark unread / read             |
| `/`                       | Search                         |
| `,`                       | Settings                       |
| `Ctrl`/`⌘`+`Enter`        | Send (while writing)           |

## Settings

Theme, density, the reading pane, signatures, automatic replies, forwarding and blocked senders are all in
**Settings** (at the bottom of the sidebar, or press `,`). See [Webmail settings](/docs/webmail-settings).

## Remote images are blocked

Many messages, especially newsletters, load their pictures from the sender's servers when you open them. That
tells the sender when you opened the message, roughly where you are, and which device you use — the same trick
tracking pixels rely on.

So the webmail **never loads remote images**. You'll see a striped placeholder where an image would be and a note
saying how many were blocked. Tiny tracking pixels are removed entirely. Pictures that are part of the message
itself (sent inside it) still show.

Loading remote images on request, through our privacy proxy so the sender still can't see you, is coming soon.

## How messages are kept safe

Every message is cleaned on our servers before you see it: scripts, forms, embedded frames and anything that
could run code or reach out to the internet are removed. The cleaned message is then shown in an isolated frame
that can't run code or reach the rest of the page. Links open in a new tab and don't tell the site where you came
from.
