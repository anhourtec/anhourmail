title: If your account is suspended

We suspend an account or a person's sign-in only to stop harm — usually spam, abuse or phishing sent from it — or
when the law or an unpaid bill requires it. A suspension never deletes anything.

## How you find out

- The account's Owners and administrators get an email (or, for a person's sign-in, that person does) with the
  reason and how to appeal.
- Signing in to the console shows a notice saying the account is suspended, with the reason.
- If only your own sign-in is suspended, the sign-in page tells you so.

## What a suspension stops

**An account suspension:**

- Its mailboxes can't be signed in to — not in the webmail and not in mail apps — so they can't send.
- Mail sent to its mailboxes and aliases is returned to the sender.
- Its people can still sign in to the console, but they see only the suspension notice and their own Security page.

**A suspension of one person's sign-in:**

- They can't sign in to the console, and any signed-in sessions are ended.
- The mailboxes assigned to them are suspended the same way. The rest of the account keeps working.

## What it keeps

Everything stored stays: your mail, folders, contacts, aliases, domains and settings. Nothing is deleted while an
account is suspended.

## How to appeal

Email <Value of="supportEmail" /> from your sign-in address and tell us why you think the suspension is a mistake.
A person reads every appeal.

## When it's lifted

You get an email, and everything comes back within a minute or two: sign-in, sending and receiving. Mailboxes that
your own account had suspended before stay suspended until you resume them.
