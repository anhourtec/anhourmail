---
title: Catch-all
description: Send mail for addresses that don't exist at your domain to one mailbox, and when not to.
---

Normally, mail sent to an address that doesn't exist at your domain is refused, and the sender is told. With a
**catch-all**, it goes to one mailbox you choose instead: `anything@your-domain` reaches you even if you never created
that address.

Catch-all is part of the Plus and Supporter plans, on your own domains. It's off until you turn it on.

<Callout title="Expect more spam">
  Spammers send to made-up names to find domains that accept everything, so a catch-all mailbox gets far more spam. If
  you give out different addresses to different sites, [aliases and masked aliases](/docs/aliases) are usually the
  better choice: you can turn each one off on its own.
</Callout>

## Turn it on

1. Open **Domains** and choose the domain. It must be verified (shown as **Active**).
2. On the **Overview** tab, under **Catch-all**, pick the mailbox that should receive the mail.
3. Choose **Turn on catch-all**. It takes effect within a minute.

Owners and Domain Administrators can change it. The chosen mailbox must be active and in the same account.

## Turn it off

Under **Catch-all**, choose **Turn off**. Mail to addresses that don't exist is refused again.

The catch-all also turns itself off when its mailbox is suspended or deleted, so mail isn't accepted for a mailbox that
can't receive it. Resuming or restoring the mailbox doesn't turn it back on: choose it again if you still want it.

## Good to know

- Real addresses and aliases at the domain always win: the catch-all only gets mail for addresses that don't exist.
- Replies from the catch-all mailbox are sent from its own address, not the made-up one the mail was sent to.
- Every change is recorded in your account's [audit log](/docs/audit-log).
