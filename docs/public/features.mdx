AnhourMail is private, low-cost email hosting: a free address at <Value of="productDomain" />, or your own domain on
the paid plans, with fast open-source webmail and every standard mail protocol. This page lists what works today,
grouped the way people look for it, with a link to the guide for each. What isn't built yet is listed at the end.

## Email on your own domain

- **Custom domain email** on Plus and Supporter: add a domain you own and keep it with your registrar; you only add
  DNS records.
- **Guided DNS setup.** The console shows every record with a copy button and checks each one live, so you can see
  what's left. It works with any DNS provider.
- **Signed mail.** We create DKIM keys for your domain (an RSA key and an Ed25519 key) and show them as
  ready-to-paste records, alongside SPF and DMARC.
- **Catch-all** sends mail for addresses that don't exist at your domain to one mailbox.
- **Automatic setup records** (optional) let many mail apps fill in the server settings from your address.

Guides: [Use your own domain](/docs/custom-domain) · [Catch-all](/docs/custom-domain/catch-all)

## Email aliases and masked aliases

- **Aliases** are extra addresses that deliver to your mailbox, such as hello@ or shop@ on your domain.
- **Masked aliases** are random addresses you give to one site each, so the site never learns your real address.
- **Switch any alias off** when it starts getting spam, and back on later. A note on each reminds you where you used
  it. Reply from whichever address the mail came to.
- **Plus addressing:** add `+anything` after your name, with no setup.
- **How many:** on Free, <Value of="aliasesFree" /> aliases and <Value of="maskedAliasesFree" /> masked aliases at a
  time; on the paid plans, up to <Value of="aliasesPerMailbox" /> per mailbox, both kinds together.

Guide: [Aliases and masked aliases](/docs/aliases)

## Private by default

- **No ads, no tracking, no profiling.** Paid plans fund the service, and there's no analytics in the product or on
  this site.
- **Remote images are blocked** until you choose to load them, so tracking pixels can't report when you open a
  message. When you do load them, they come through our privacy proxy, so the sender never sees your IP address.
- **Every message opens in a sandboxed frame** with scripts off, after its HTML has been cleaned.
- **Your mail stays out of our logs.** Subjects, bodies and recipients never go into logs, analytics or the audit
  log.

Guides: [Remote images](/docs/webmail#remote-images-are-blocked-until-you-load-them) ·
[How messages are kept safe](/docs/webmail#how-messages-are-kept-safe)

## Fast, open-source webmail

The webmail at <Value of="webmailHost" /> talks JMAP to the mail server, so it fetches only what changed instead of
downloading whole folders.

- **Conversations and folders:** threads, archive, junk, move, pin favourite folders, and search with filters.
- **Writing:** rich text (sent with a plain-text copy), drafts, undo send, attachments, and named signatures.
- **Several mailboxes in one session,** and shared mailboxes opened by address in a tab of their own.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** for everything you do often.
- **Settings that run on the server:** automatic replies, confirmed forwarding, and blocked and safe senders, so they
  apply in every app you use.
- **Your look:** light and dark themes, density, reading pane, your photo and sender name.

Guides: [Webmail](/docs/webmail) · [Webmail settings](/docs/webmail-settings) ·
[Folders and signatures](/docs/folders-and-signatures)

## Families and small teams

On Plus and Supporter, one account holds everyone, for one yearly price.

- **People and roles:** invite up to <Value of="maxPeople" /> people and give each a role (owner, domain, user or
  billing administrator, member, or read-only).
- **Mailboxes:** <Value of="includedMailboxes" /> included, sharing the account's <Value of="plusStorage" />. Add a
  user and their mailbox in one go, set storage per mailbox, and suspend, delete or restore mailboxes.
- **Shared mailboxes** for addresses like support@ or family@, on your own domain or ours.
- **Delegation:** give someone **Read and manage**, **Send as** or **Send on behalf** on a mailbox, and convert a
  person's mailbox to a shared one when they leave, so no mail is lost.

Guides: [People and roles](/docs/people-and-roles) · [Mailboxes](/docs/mailboxes)

## Sign-in security

Two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app, recovery codes, and passkeys or security keys. Owners can require a
second factor for everyone in the account, and you can see and end your sessions.

Guide: [Sign-in security](/docs/sign-in-security)

## Accountability

An append-only, tamper-evident audit log records every change in your account and who made it. When Anhourtec support
needs to look at your console, it's read-only, short, explained and shown in your audit log.

Guide: [Audit log](/docs/audit-log)

## Your data, and leaving

- **Export your mail and contacts** whenever you like: one `.mbox` file per folder and a vCard file, straight from
  your mailbox. Account owners can also export the account's settings and audit log.
- **Delete your account** with a <Value of="accountDeletionGraceDays" />-day grace period you can cancel.

Guide: [Export your data and delete your account](/docs/export-and-deletion)

## Open source and self-hosting

Stalwart, run unmodified. You can read the code, and run the stack yourself.
