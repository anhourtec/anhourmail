title: Audit log
description: See every change made in your account, who made it and when, including anything Anhourtec staff did and when support opened it.
---

The audit log lists every change in your account: domains added, mailboxes created or suspended, aliases, invites,
role changes, refused attempts and more. Entries can't be edited or deleted. Owners, User Administrators and
Read-only (Auditor) people can open it from **Audit log** in the console.

## What each entry shows

- **When**, in UTC.
- **Who**: the person's sign-in address and role, **System** for automatic work (for example a deleted mailbox being
  removed for good after its grace period), or **Anhourtec staff**.
- **What** happened, and to what (a domain, a mailbox, a person).
- **Result**: success, denied (the person wasn't allowed) or failed.
- **IP**: only the network part, like `203.0.113.x`.

Select an entry to see its details. Passwords, links from emails, secrets and mail content are never recorded.

## Anhourtec staff

If anyone at Anhourtec looks at or changes your account, for example while helping you with a support request, it
appears in your audit log as **Anhourtec staff** with their role. Staff can't read your mail: there is no way to do it
from our tools.

## Anhourtec support access

To help with a support request, an Anhourtec support person may need to see your console the way your admins see it,
for example to find out why a domain isn't verifying. This is called support access. It is:

- **Read-only.** They see your settings, people, domains and mailboxes, but they can't change anything, can't open
  webmail and can't read your mail. Billing, deleting the account, roles, passwords and two-factor sign-in stay yours.
- **Short.** It lasts <Value of="actAsMinutes" /> minutes. They can extend it, giving a new reason each time, but
  never past <Value of="actAsMaxHours" /> hours in all.
- **Explained.** They must write down why they need it, and confirm it's them before it starts. Accounts of
  Anhourtec staff can't be opened this way.
- **Recorded.** Your audit log shows **Anhourtec support access started** with the reason they gave, every change
  they tried (refused, because it's read-only), and **Anhourtec support access ended**. These entries are listed as
  **Anhourtec staff · Support access**; choose **Anhourtec staff** in the filters to see only them.
- **Announced.** Your account's Owners get an email when it starts, with the reason and a link to the audit log.

If you see support access you didn't ask for, write to <Value of="supportEmail" /> and tell us.

## Filters

Narrow the list with the filters above it: area (for example **Domains** or **Account and people**), who did it (a
person, Anhourtec staff or the system), result and dates, then choose **Apply filters**. **Older** shows earlier
entries and **Newest entries** goes back to the start.
