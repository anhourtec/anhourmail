---
title: Audit log
description: See every change made in your account, who made it and when, including anything Anhourtec staff did.
---

The audit log lists every change in your account: domains added, mailboxes created or suspended, aliases, invites,
role changes, refused attempts and more. Entries can't be edited or deleted. Owners, User Administrators and
Read-only (Auditor) people can open it from **Audit log** in the console.

## What each entry shows

- **When**, in UTC.
- **Who**: the person's sign-in address and role, **System** for automatic work (for example a deleted mailbox being
  removed for good after its grace period), or **Anhourtec staff**.
- **What** happened, and to what (a domain, a mailbox, a person).
- **Result**: success, denied (the person wasn't allowed) or failed.
- **IP**: only the network part, like `203.0.113.x`.

Select an entry to see its details. Passwords, links from emails, secrets and mail content are never recorded.

## Anhourtec staff

If anyone at Anhourtec looks at or changes your account, for example while helping you with a support request, it
appears in your audit log as **Anhourtec staff** with their role. Staff can't read your mail: there is no way to do it
from our tools.

## Filters

Narrow the list by area (for example **Domains** or **Account and people**), by who did it (a person, Anhourtec staff
or the system), by result, and by date. **Older** shows earlier entries.
