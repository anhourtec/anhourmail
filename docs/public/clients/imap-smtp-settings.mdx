## Your password

Mail apps don't use your account password. Create an [app password](/docs/clients/app-passwords) for each app on the
console's **App passwords** page, then paste it into the app. If you lose a device, revoke its app password and nothing
else changes.

<Callout title="Two-step verification">
  App passwords keep working when two-step verification is on, which is why mail apps use them.
</Callout>

## If the app can't connect

1. Check that the username is your full email address.
2. Check that you pasted an app password, not your account password.
3. Check the security setting matches the table above for the port you chose.
