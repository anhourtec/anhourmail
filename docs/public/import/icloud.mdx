---
title: Import from iCloud Mail
description: Make an app-specific password for your Apple Account with two-factor authentication, then copy your iCloud Mail into AnhourMail.
---

To copy your mail from iCloud Mail, you make an **app-specific password** for your Apple Account and give it to the
import. iCloud Mail isn't changed: we only read it.

## Make an app-specific password

Apple only offers app-specific passwords when **two-factor authentication** is on for your Apple Account. It's on for
most accounts already.

1. Sign in to your Apple Account on the web (account.apple.com), or open your Apple Account settings on an iPhone,
   iPad or Mac.
2. Open **Sign-In and Security**, then **App-Specific Passwords**.
3. Choose to make a new one, give it a name you'll recognise, like "AnhourMail import", and create it.
4. Apple shows the password once. Copy it: you'll paste it into the import.

Apple changes its menus from time to time, so the names above may differ a little.

## Start the import

1. In the console, open **Import mail** and choose **New import**.
2. Under **Provider**, choose **iCloud Mail**. The server settings are filled in for you:

<ImportServerTable preset="icloud" />

3. Type your iCloud Mail address as the **User name**, paste the **App-specific password** into **App password**, and
   choose **Start import**.

Your folders, Sent, Drafts and Archive arrive in the matching places here. The rest is the same for every provider:
see [Move your mail here](/docs/import).

## If iCloud refuses the sign-in

The import says **Needs you: the old provider refused the sign-in**. Check:

- You used the app-specific password, not your Apple Account password.
- The user name is your **iCloud Mail** address (the one ending in an iCloud domain). The email address you sign in to
  your Apple Account with can be a different one.
- If it still fails, Apple also accepts only the part of your iCloud Mail address before the @.

Then choose **Resume** and type the app-specific password again (or a new one).

When you're done, revoke the app-specific password in your Apple Account settings.
