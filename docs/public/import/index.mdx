title: Move your mail here
description: Copy your old mailbox's mail, folders, read and flagged marks and dates into AnhourMail with an app password. The old mailbox is only read, and nothing there changes.
---

You can copy everything from an old mailbox at another provider into a mailbox here: your mail, your folders, which
messages are read or flagged, and their original dates. We sign in to the old mailbox with an **app password**, read
it, and copy what we find. Nothing at the old provider is changed, moved or deleted.

Contacts move separately, as a file: see [Import contacts](/docs/import/contacts).

## Before you start

- **An app password at the old provider.** Most providers don't let other apps sign in with your usual password. Make
  an app password there just for the import. The guide for your provider shows how.
- **Room in the mailbox here.** The mail needs to fit in the mailbox's storage (see
  [Storage used](/docs/mailboxes#storage-used)). If it doesn't, the import waits and tells you.
- **Your console password.** Starting an import asks for it if you haven't confirmed it's you in the
  last <Value of="stepUpMinutes" /> minutes.

## Start an import

1. In the console, open **Import mail** (under **Mail** in the left navigation), then choose **New import**.
2. Under **Import into**, pick the mailbox here that the mail should go into.
3. Under **Provider**, pick your old provider. For one that isn't listed, choose **Other IMAP server** and type its
   **IMAP server** and **Port** (see [Import from any IMAP server](/docs/import/imap)).
4. Type the **User name** for the old mailbox (usually its full email address) and the **App password** you made
   there.
5. Check the options (below), then choose **Start import**.

The import runs on our servers: you can close the page, sign out or turn off your computer. When it finishes, we email
you a summary with how much was copied into each folder and how long it took.

### Options

- **Leave out Junk and Deleted items** (on to start with). Turn it off to copy those too. Copied Junk arrives as an
  ordinary folder named **Imported Junk**, kept apart from your Junk folder here.
- **Put the old folders under Imported.** Your own folders go into one folder called **Imported**, so they don't mix
  with folders you already have. Inbox, Sent and Drafts still go to their usual places.
- **Keep copying new mail until I stop it.** After the first copy, new mail at the old provider keeps being copied
  here every <Value of="importSyncEveryMinutes" /> minutes, for up to <Value of="importSyncDays" /> days. Use it while
  you switch over: see [Switch a domain without losing mail](/docs/import/switching-a-domain).

## What's copied

- **Folders**, with their folders inside folders. The old Inbox, Sent, Drafts and Archive go into the matching folders
  here; every other folder keeps its name.
- **An "All mail" folder**, on providers that have one, is copied last, into **Archive**, and only the messages that
  weren't already copied from another folder. So nothing arrives twice.
- **Read, flagged and replied-to marks**, and labels (keywords) that mail apps set.
- **The original dates**, so your mail sorts the way it did before.

## What isn't copied

- Messages over <Value of="importMaxMessageSize" />. They stay at the old provider, and the import counts them as
  **Too large**.
- Messages that were deleted at the old provider but not yet removed for good.
- Folders like "Starred" or "Important" that only show mail kept in other folders.
- The old provider's spam-filter training. Your spam filter here learns from what you do here.
- Contacts and calendars. Contacts import as a file: see [Import contacts](/docs/import/contacts).

Mail that's already in the mailbox here isn't copied twice. That makes it safe to run an import again, and safe to
forward mail from the old account while you switch.

## Follow an import

The **Import mail** page lists every import with its status and progress. Open one to see more:

- **Status:** **Waiting to start**, **Checking the old mailbox**, **Copying**, **Paused**, **Needs you** (with the
  reason), **Copying new mail** (when it keeps copying new mail), **Finished**, **Finished early** (it stopped before
  copying everything; the page says why), **Cancelled** or **Ended** (something went wrong; the page says what).
- **Counts:** **Copied**, **Already there (skipped)**, **Too large** and **Not accepted** (messages the mail server
  here refused, for example because they were damaged).
- **Folders:** the folders it found, and how many messages each one holds. While it runs, only the folder names are
  shown.
- **Buttons:** **Pause** and **Resume**, **Stop syncing** (when it keeps copying new mail), and **Cancel import**,
  which stops it at once. Mail that's already copied stays.

Up to <Value of="importConcurrency" /> imports run at the same time in one account. Start more and they wait their
turn. Each mailbox has one import at a time.

### When it says "Needs you"

An import that can't carry on waits for you, and the page says why and what to do. Fix it, then choose **Resume**. If
the old provider refused the app password, you can type a new one when you resume.

## Your privacy

- The old mailbox is only read. We never delete, move or mark anything there, and messages you hadn't read stay unread.
- The app password is kept encrypted while the import runs, and deleted as soon as it ends: when it finishes, or you
  cancel it. Nobody can see it after you type it, our staff included.
- We keep counts and folder names only while the import runs. Once it's done we keep the counts, never the folder
  names, subjects or anything else about your mail.
- Imports don't send mail and don't count toward your [sending limits](/docs/sending-limits).
- An imported message keeps the sender checks the old provider made. We didn't check it ourselves when it arrived, so
  the webmail says so in its details ("Imported; not checked by AnhourMail"), and a safe sender's images don't load in
  it by themselves.

## Who can import

- **Everyone but Auditors** can import into the mailboxes that are theirs.
- **Owners** and **User Administrators** can also import into other people's mailboxes and shared mailboxes. Only an
  Owner can import into an Owner's or administrator's mailbox. When an admin starts an import into your mailbox, we
  email you, and you can see it and stop it on your own **Import mail** page.
- Admins can start imports for many mailboxes at once from a CSV file: see
  [Import many mailboxes](/docs/import/many-mailboxes).

## Providers we can't import from yet

Some providers only let other apps in through their own sign-in page, not with an app password. That's the case for
business mailboxes at some of the largest providers, where password sign-in for mail apps is turned off. We can't
import from those mailboxes yet. If your provider offers app passwords, or lets you turn on IMAP with a password, you
can import now.
