---
title: Import from Yahoo Mail
description: Make a Yahoo Mail app password in your account security settings, then copy your Yahoo mail and folders into AnhourMail.
---

To copy your mail from Yahoo Mail, you make an **app password** in your Yahoo account and give it to the import.
Yahoo Mail isn't changed: we only read it.

## Make an app password

1. Sign in to Yahoo and open your account settings (**Account info**), then **Account security**.
2. Choose **Generate app password** (it may be called **Manage app passwords**).
3. Type a name you'll recognise, like "AnhourMail import", and generate it.
4. Yahoo shows the password once. Copy it: you'll paste it into the import.

Yahoo changes its menus from time to time, so the names above may differ a little. Searching Yahoo's help for "app
password" finds the current steps.

## Start the import

1. In the console, open **Import mail** and choose **New import**.
2. Under **Provider**, choose **Yahoo Mail**. The server settings are filled in for you:

<ImportServerTable preset="yahoo" />

3. Type your full Yahoo Mail address as the **User name**, paste the **App password**, and choose **Start import**.

Your folders, Sent, Drafts and Archive arrive in the matching places here. The rest is the same for every provider:
see [Move your mail here](/docs/import).

## If Yahoo refuses the sign-in

The import says **Needs you: the old provider refused the sign-in**. Check that you used the app password, not your
usual Yahoo password, and your full Yahoo Mail address as the user name. Then make a new app password and choose
**Resume** with it.

## New mail while you switch

Not every Yahoo Mail plan can forward mail to another address. If yours can't, turn on **Keep copying new mail until I
stop it** when you start the import: new mail at Yahoo keeps being copied here until you stop it. See
[Switch a domain without losing mail](/docs/import/switching-a-domain).

When you're done, delete the app password in Yahoo's account security settings.
