title: Import from Gmail
description: Make a Gmail app password with 2-Step Verification, then copy your Gmail mail, labels and read marks into AnhourMail.
---

To copy your mail from Gmail, you make an **app password** in your Google Account and give it to the import. Gmail
isn't changed: we only read it.

## Make an app password

Google only offers app passwords when **2-Step Verification** is on for your account.

1. Sign in to your Google Account (myaccount.google.com) and open **Security**.
2. If **2-Step Verification** is off, turn it on first and follow the steps.
3. Search for **App passwords** with the search box at the top of your Google Account (it's also listed under
   2-Step Verification).
4. Type a name you'll recognise, like "AnhourMail import", and create it.
5. Google shows a 16-letter password once. Copy it: you'll paste it into the import. The spaces don't matter.

Google changes its menus from time to time, so the names above may differ a little. If you don't see **App passwords**
at all, see [If Gmail refuses the sign-in](#if-gmail-refuses-the-sign-in).

## Start the import

1. In the console, open **Import mail** and choose **New import**.
2. Under **Provider**, choose **Gmail**. The server settings are filled in for you:

3. Type your full Gmail address as the **User name**, paste the **App password**, and choose **Start import**.

The rest is the same for every provider: see [Move your mail here](/docs/import).

## How Gmail's labels arrive

Gmail shows labels to mail apps as folders, and the import copies them that way:

- Each label becomes a folder here, with labels inside labels kept as folders inside folders.
- A message with several labels is copied into each of those folders, the way a mail app sees it.
- **Sent Mail**, **Drafts**, **Spam** and **Trash** go into Sent, Drafts, Junk and Deleted items here. Spam and Trash
  are left out unless you turn off **Leave out Junk and Deleted items**.
- **All Mail** holds every message, including archived mail that has no label. It's copied last, into **Archive**, and
  only the messages not already copied from another folder. So archived mail arrives once, and nothing else twice.
- **Starred** and **Important** aren't copied as folders: those messages arrive in their own folders, and starred ones
  keep their flag.

If your Gmail settings limit how many messages a folder shows to mail apps (under **Forwarding and POP/IMAP**, **Folder
size limits**), set it to no limit before you start, or older mail won't be copied.

## If Gmail refuses the sign-in

The import says **Needs you: the old provider refused the sign-in**. Check:

- You used the app password, not your usual Google password.
- The user name is your full Gmail address.
- If your Gmail settings have an **IMAP access** option under **Forwarding and POP/IMAP**, it's turned on.
- **No App passwords option?** 2-Step Verification must be on. On an account managed by your work or school, the
  administrator may not allow app passwords or IMAP. Ask them; if they can't turn it on, we can't import from that
  account yet (see [Providers we can't import from yet](/docs/import#providers-we-cant-import-from-yet)).

Then make a new app password and choose **Resume** with it.

## When you're done

Revoke the app password in your Google Account (**App passwords**, then delete it). The import has already deleted its
copy, but it's good practice.
