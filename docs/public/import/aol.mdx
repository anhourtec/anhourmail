---
title: Import from AOL Mail
description: Make an AOL Mail app password in your account security settings, then copy your AOL mail and folders into AnhourMail.
---

To copy your mail from AOL Mail, you make an **app password** in your AOL account and give it to the import. AOL Mail
isn't changed: we only read it.

## Make an app password

1. Sign in to AOL and open your account settings (**Account info**), then **Account security**.
2. Choose **Generate app password** (it may be called **Generate and manage app passwords**).
3. Type a name you'll recognise, like "AnhourMail import", and generate it.
4. AOL shows the password once. Copy it: you'll paste it into the import.

AOL changes its menus from time to time, so the names above may differ a little. Searching AOL's help for "app
password" finds the current steps.

## Start the import

1. In the console, open **Import mail** and choose **New import**.
2. Under **Provider**, choose **AOL Mail**. The server settings are filled in for you:

<ImportServerTable preset="aol" />

3. Type your full AOL address as the **User name**, paste the **App password**, and choose **Start import**.

Your folders, Sent, Drafts and Archive arrive in the matching places here. The rest is the same for every provider:
see [Move your mail here](/docs/import).

## If AOL refuses the sign-in

The import says **Needs you: the old provider refused the sign-in**. Check that you used the app password, not your
usual AOL password, and your full AOL address as the user name. Then make a new app password and choose **Resume**
with it.

When you're done, delete the app password in AOL's account security settings.
