---
title: Switch a domain without losing mail
description: Move your own domain's mail to AnhourMail step by step - copy the old mailbox, keep new mail coming while DNS changes, point MX here, then stop syncing.
---

When your domain's mail moves from another provider to AnhourMail, mail keeps arriving at the old provider until DNS
points here. These steps make sure nothing is lost in between, and nothing arrives twice.

## 1. Add the domain and its mailboxes

[Add your domain](/docs/custom-domain) in the console and create a mailbox for each address (see
[Mailboxes](/docs/mailboxes)). Don't change the domain's MX record yet: until you do, mail keeps going to the old
provider.

## 2. Start the imports with "Keep copying new mail"

For each mailbox, [start an import](/docs/import#start-an-import) and turn on **Keep copying new mail until I stop
it**. (Admins moving a team can do them all at once, with `sync` set to `yes`: see
[Import many mailboxes](/docs/import/many-mailboxes).)

After the first full copy, the import shows **Copying new mail**: every <Value of="importSyncEveryMinutes" /> minutes
it copies whatever has arrived at the old provider since. It keeps going until you choose **Stop syncing**, for up
to <Value of="importSyncDays" /> days from the start.

## 3. Forward from the old account (optional)

If the old provider can forward mail automatically, you can also turn that on, to the new address here. Forwarded mail
arrives straight away, and the import's next pass skips it, because the same message is already in the mailbox. So
forwarding and syncing together never give you two copies.

## 4. Point the domain here

Change the domain's DNS records to the ones the console shows, starting with **MX**. The steps and the records are in
[Use your own domain](/docs/custom-domain). DNS changes can take a while to reach everyone, so for a few hours some
mail may still go to the old provider. The import copies it here.

When the console has checked your MX record, the import page says: **Your domain's mail now arrives here.**

## 5. Stop syncing

Wait until nothing new is arriving at the old provider, for example for a day. Then open each import and choose
**Stop syncing**. It copies anything that's left one last time, then shows **Finished**, deletes the app password it
held, and we email you a summary. It doesn't stop on its own when MX changes, so you decide when it's done. If you
forget, it stops by itself <Value of="importSyncDays" /> days after the import started.

## 6. Tidy up

- Turn off forwarding at the old provider, if you turned it on.
- Revoke the app passwords you made for the import.
- Set up your mail apps with the new settings: see [Mail app settings](/docs/clients/imap-smtp-settings).
- Bring your contacts too: see [Import contacts](/docs/import/contacts).

Keep the old account for a while before you close it, in case you need something that wasn't copied, like messages
over <Value of="importMaxMessageSize" />.
