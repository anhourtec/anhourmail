## Is AnhourMail end-to-end encrypted?

No. Mail is encrypted in transit between servers and apps, and stored on our own servers. Mail between
different providers can't be end-to-end encrypted without both sides using the same tool, so we don't claim it.
The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) explains what we store and for how long.

## Can I export my mail?

Yes, at any time, in standard formats that other providers and mail apps can import: in the webmail, **Settings →
Export** downloads every folder as an mbox file and your contacts as a vCard file. The
[Terms of Service](/legal/terms) promise this on every plan. See
[Export your data and delete your account](/docs/export-and-deletion).

## What happens when I delete my account?

Its mailboxes are suspended at once, and after a grace period of <Value of="accountDeletionGraceDays" /> days, in
which an Owner can cancel, its mail, contacts and settings are deleted. Billing records we are legally required to
keep are kept for the time the law requires and nothing more. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for every retention period.

## How much storage do I get?

Free accounts get <Value of="freeStorage" /> for their one mailbox. Plus and Supporter cost the same for the whole
account, however many people are in it, and include <Value of="plusStorage" /> shared by the whole account: you decide
how much each mailbox gets. More storage, +<Value of="storageBlock" /> for <Value of="storageBlockPrice" /> a year, and
extra mailboxes, <Value of="extraMailboxPrice" /> a year each, come with billing, which isn't open yet. Nothing is ever
charged automatically. See [Mailboxes](/docs/mailboxes#account-storage).

## What happens when a mailbox is full?

New mail to it is refused with a "mailbox full" bounce, so the sender knows it didn't arrive. Nothing is deleted. The
console warns at 80% and 95%. Delete some mail, give the mailbox a bigger share of the account's storage, or (once
billing opens) add more.

## What happens to a free account nobody uses?

A free account nobody signs in to for <Value of="freeInactivityMonths" /> months gets email warnings, then is
suspended. Its mail is kept for <Value of="inactiveMailKeptDays" /> more days, and its address is never given to anyone
else. Paid accounts are never closed for inactivity.

## Do you read my mail?

No. Staff support tools never show mail content. Any staff action on your account appears in your account's
activity log.

## What can't I send?

Bulk, newsletter and marketing mail, spam, phishing and malware. The [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use)
has the full list and explains how we enforce it.

## Who runs AnhourMail?

Anhourtec, a small independent software company. The code is open source, so you can see exactly how it works.
