title: People and roles
description: Invite family or a small group into your account, choose what each person can do, and remove people.
---

On the Plus and Supporter plans an account can have up to <Value of="maxPeople" /> people, counting invites that are
still waiting. The Free plan is for one person. Open **People and roles** in the console to see everyone, their role,
when they joined, and the invites you've sent.

A person is someone who can sign in to the console; a mailbox is an address with mail in it. The quickest way to add
someone is [**Add a user**](/docs/mailboxes#add-a-user) on the **Mailboxes** page: it creates their mailbox and their
login together, and they show up here. Use **Invite someone** for a person who should sign in with an address they
already have, like an outside admin. You can also give an existing person another mailbox on the **Mailboxes** page by
choosing them under **Belongs to**.

## Roles

| Role                  | Can                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner                 | Everything, including inviting, changing and removing other owners and admins.        |
| Domain Administrator  | Add, verify and remove domains.                                                       |
| User Administrator    | Create and manage mailboxes, manage everyone's aliases, and invite or remove Members. |
| Billing Administrator | Change the plan and see invoices.                                                     |
| Member                | Their own mailbox, aliases and settings.                                              |
| Read-only (Auditor)   | See settings, people, domains, mailboxes and the audit log. Changes nothing.          |

## Invite someone

1. Open **People and roles** and choose **Invite someone**. A panel opens on the right.
2. Enter their email address and choose a role. Owners can choose any role; User Administrators invite Members.
3. Choose **Send invite**.

We email them a link. It works once, only for that address, for <Value of="inviteDays" /> days. When they open it:

- if they don't have an AnhourMail login yet, they enter their name and choose a password, and they're in;
- if they already have one, they sign in with that address and join.

Pending invites are listed under **Pending invites**; their ⋮ menu has **Resend invite** and **Revoke invite…**.
Resending sends a new link with a fresh

<Value of="inviteDays" /> days (the old link stops working). Revoking an invite stops its link at once. An account can
send up to <Value of="inviteEmailsPerDay" /> invite emails a day, resends included.

## Profile details

Job title, department, office, phone numbers and a postal address are optional details kept with each person, for the
people who run the account. Choose someone's name and open the **Profile** tab to see them; **Edit profile** changes
them (clear a field to remove it). Everyone can edit their own, from **Profile** in the account menu (your picture or
initials, top right) or their own **Profile** tab. User Administrators can edit Members' details; an owner's or an
admin's need an owner. The [audit log](/docs/audit-log) records which details changed, never what they say.

## Change a role or remove someone

Choose someone's name to open their details panel. Owners change a role on its **Role** tab (or with **Change role**
in the ⋮ menu beside the name). User Administrators can remove Members. Removing someone asks you to confirm, and they lose access to the account in the console straight away. Their mailboxes
and mail are kept; suspend or delete a mailbox from the **Mailboxes** page if you need to. The
[app passwords](/docs/clients/app-passwords) on their mailboxes are revoked, so their mail apps stop signing in.

Some changes are never allowed, so an account can't lock itself out:

- an account always has at least one owner: the last owner can't be removed or given another role;
- nobody can change their own role or remove themselves;
- only an owner can invite, change or remove an owner or an admin.

Every invite, role change and removal is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/audit-log).
