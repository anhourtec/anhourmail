---
title: People and roles
description: Invite family or a small group into your account, choose what each person can do, and remove people.
---

On the Plus and Supporter plans an account can have up to <Value of="maxPeople" /> people, counting invites that are
still waiting. The Free plan is for one person. Open **People and roles** in the console to see everyone, their role,
when they joined, and the invites you've sent.

A person is someone who can sign in to the console; a mailbox is an address with mail in it. The quickest way to add
someone is [**Add a user**](/docs/mailboxes#add-a-user) on the **Mailboxes** page: it creates their mailbox and their
login together, and they show up here. Use **Invite someone** for a person who should sign in with an address they
already have, like an outside admin. You can also give an existing person another mailbox on the **Mailboxes** page by
choosing them under **Belongs to**.

## Roles

| Role                  | Can                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner                 | Everything, including inviting, changing and removing other owners and admins.        |
| Domain Administrator  | Add, verify and remove domains.                                                       |
| User Administrator    | Create and manage mailboxes, manage everyone's aliases, and invite or remove Members. |
| Billing Administrator | Change the plan and see invoices.                                                     |
| Member                | Their own mailbox, aliases and settings.                                              |
| Read-only (Auditor)   | See settings, people, domains, mailboxes and the audit log. Changes nothing.          |

Anhourtec support never appears in this list. When support needs to look at your account, they open it read-only for
a short time, you're emailed, and every step is in your audit log: see
[Anhourtec support access](/docs/audit-log#anhourtec-support-access).

## Invite someone

1. Open **People and roles** and choose **Invite someone**. A panel opens on the right.
2. Enter their email address and choose a role. Owners can choose any role; User Administrators invite Members.
3. Choose **Send invite**.

We email them a link. It works once, only for that address, for <Value of="inviteDays" /> days. When they open it:

- if they don't have an AnhourMail login yet, they enter their name and choose a password, and they're in;
- if they already have one, they sign in with that address and join.

Pending invites are listed under **Pending invites**; their ⋮ menu has **Resend invite** and **Revoke invite…**.
Resending sends a new link that works for another <Value of="inviteDays" /> days (the old link stops working).
Revoking an invite stops its link at once. An account can send up to <Value of="inviteEmailsPerDay" /> invite emails
a day, resends included.

When the account already has <Value of="maxPeople" /> people, counting pending invites, the **People and roles**
page says so instead of offering **Invite someone**, and the new-invite and **Add a user** buttons in the navigation
are greyed out with the reason. Remove someone or revoke an invite to make room.

## Profile details

Job title, department, office, phone numbers and a postal address are optional details kept with each person, for the
people who run the account. Choose someone's name and open the **Profile** tab to see them; **Edit profile** changes
them (clear a field to remove it). Everyone can edit their own, from **Profile** in the account menu (your picture or
initials, top right) or their own **Profile** tab. User Administrators can edit Members' details; an owner's or an
admin's need an owner. Someone who also belongs to another account edits their details themself: they are the same in
every account, so no one account's admins can change them. The [audit log](/docs/audit-log) records which details
changed, never what they say.

## Profile photos

Each person has one profile photo, shown wherever they appear in the account: the console's top bar and account menu,
**People and roles**, and the webmail of their own mailboxes, next to mail from them. Without one, their initials
show. Add yours on **Profile** in the account menu with **Upload photo** (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to

<Value of="photoMaxUpload" />
); **Change photo** replaces it and **Remove** goes back to initials. We crop it to a square and save a small copy of
our own, without the original's location or camera details, so the file you chose never leaves your device as it was.
You can also change it in the webmail, under **Settings**, **Account**: it's the same photo.

The same people who can edit someone's [profile details](#profile-details) can change their photo, on the **Profile**
tab of their details panel. A shared mailbox with no person behind it can have its own photo: choose it on the
[**Mailboxes**](/docs/mailboxes) page and use **Upload photo** under **Photo** (owners and User Administrators).
Photos are only shown to people in the account, never to anyone outside it, and the [audit log](/docs/audit-log)
records when one changed.

Your name in the console and the name people see on mail you send are separate: the sender name is set for each
mailbox in the webmail ([Settings, Account](/docs/webmail-settings#account)).

## Mailboxes someone can open

A person's details panel lists, under **Mailboxes they can open** on its **Account** tab, every mailbox in the account
they've been [given access to](/docs/mailboxes#give-people-access-to-a-mailbox), with what they may do in each
(**Read and manage**, **Send as**, **Send on behalf**). Choose an address to open that mailbox's **Delegation** tab.
Owners, User Administrators and Auditors see this list.

## Change a role or remove someone

Choose someone's name to open their details panel. Owners change a role on its **Role** tab (or with **Change role**
in the ⋮ menu beside the name). User Administrators can remove Members. Removing someone asks you to confirm, and they
lose access to the account in the console straight away.

Their mailboxes and mail are kept, and mail keeps arriving. Each of their mailboxes becomes a
[shared mailbox](/docs/mailboxes#add-a-shared-mailbox) that belongs to nobody, and their sign-in to it is turned off:
we give it a new password that nobody is shown, so they are signed out of the webmail, and the
[app passwords](/docs/clients/app-passwords) on it are revoked, so their mail apps stop signing in.

When you remove one person who has a mailbox, on the Plus and Supporter plans, the dialog can also hand their mail
over:

1. Under **Give full access to their mailbox to**, choose who should have **Read and manage** on it (for example
   their manager), or leave **Nobody for now**.
2. Tick **Also let them send as it** to give **Send as** too.
3. Choose **Remove**. With someone chosen, the console may ask you to confirm it's you first. If that person can't be
   given access, nothing is removed and the dialog says why; choose **Nobody for now** to remove them without it.

You can change who has access later on the mailbox's [**Delegation** tab](/docs/mailboxes#give-people-access-to-a-mailbox).
Removing several people at once doesn't offer this step. Then decide what else happens to the mailbox on the
**Mailboxes** page: set a new password for whoever takes it over,
[make it someone's mailbox](/docs/mailboxes#make-a-shared-mailbox-a-user), or suspend or delete it. Any access the
removed person had to other mailboxes ends with the removal.

Some changes are never allowed, so an account can't lock itself out:

- an account always has at least one owner: the last owner can't be removed or given another role;
- nobody can change their own role or remove themselves;
- only an owner can invite, change or remove an owner or an admin.

Every invite, role change and removal is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/audit-log).
