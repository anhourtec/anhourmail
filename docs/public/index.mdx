AnhourMail is private, fast email. You get an address at <Value of="productDomain" />, or you can use a domain
you already own. There are no ads and no tracking, and you can take all of your mail with you whenever you like.

## Get an account

AnhourMail is invite-only while we grow. Join the waitlist on the [home page](/#waitlist) and we will email you
when your invite is ready.

## Read your mail

- **In the browser:** sign in to webmail at <Value of="webmailHost" />.
- **In a mail app:** Thunderbird, Apple Mail, Outlook and most other apps work. See
  [mail app settings](/docs/clients/imap-smtp-settings).
