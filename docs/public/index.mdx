AnhourMail is private, fast email. You get an address at <Value of="productDomain" />, or you can use a domain
you already own. There are no ads and no tracking, and you can take all of your mail with you whenever you like.

## Get an account

Create an account in the console at <Value of="appHost" />: choose **Sign up** and enter your email address and a
password. The free plan gives you an address at <Value of="productDomain" />; paid plans add your own domains. See
[pricing](/pricing).

## Find your way around the console

- **Search** at the top finds mailboxes, aliases, domains and people you can see. Press `/` to jump to it.
- **The navigation** on the left lists what your role can open, with counts (mailboxes, aliases, domains, invites
  waiting to be accepted). Its main button adds the thing you're most likely to add next. Collapse it to icons with
  the menu button at the top left, or ⌘B / Ctrl+B; on a phone it opens as a drawer.
- **Lists** have actions above them, a search box on the right, filters underneath and a ⋮ menu beside each name.
  Tick several rows to act on them together where that's possible.
- **Details** open in a panel from the right, over the list. Close it with the ✕ or Escape; its address can be
  bookmarked or shared with someone who has the same access.
- Press `?` for keyboard shortcuts.

## Read your mail

- **In the browser:** sign in to webmail at <Value of="webmailHost" />.
- **In a mail app:** Thunderbird, Apple Mail, Outlook and most other apps work. See
  [mail app settings](/docs/clients/imap-smtp-settings).
