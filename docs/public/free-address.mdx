Every account can have an address at <Value of="productDomain" />, like `jane.doe@` followed by our domain. It's a
full mailbox: use it in the [webmail](/docs/webmail) and in any [mail app](/docs/clients/imap-smtp-settings).

## Get your address

1. Sign up in the console at <Value of="appHost" />, then **confirm your email address**: we send a link to the
   address you signed up with. It works for <Value of="emailVerificationHours" /> hours. If it has expired or didn't
   arrive, choose **Send the link again** where the console asks for it.
2. On the console's home page choose **Get your free address** (it's also on the **Mailboxes** page).
3. Type the name you want. The console tells you as you type whether it's available.
4. Set the **password** you'll sign in with: type one, or choose **Generate a strong password** and copy it. It's shown
   only until you create the address.
5. Choose **Get this address**. It's ready within a minute; then sign in to the webmail with the full address and that
   password.

Mail apps use [app passwords](/docs/clients/app-passwords) rather than this password.

## Choosing a name

- <Value of="platformAddressMin" /> to <Value of="platformAddressMax" /> characters: lowercase letters, numbers, dots,
  hyphens and underscores. Capitals are fine to type; addresses are not case-sensitive.
- Start and end with a letter or a number, and don't put two dots in a row.
- Shorter names are held back for now.
- Some names are reserved so that nobody can pass themselves off as us, as a business's role address or as an
  account-security message:
  - mail and server names such as `postmaster`, `abuse`, `webmail` and `autoconfig`;
  - role addresses such as `admin`, `support`, `contact`, `hello`, `billing`, `careers`, `press` and `legal`;
  - automated senders and placeholders such as `noreply`, `notifications`, `newsletter`, `test` and `nobody`;
  - account and security words such as `security`, `verify`, `login`, `password`, `recovery` and `official`, also at
    the start or end of a longer name (`support-desk`, `bank.security`);
  - our own names, anywhere in a name.

  Look-alikes count too: `no.reply`, `post_master`, `n0reply` or `admin2` are refused.

- Names are first come, first served. A name that was used once isn't given to anyone else, even after it's deleted:
  mail meant for its old owner never reaches someone new. The account that had it can take it back.

## Deleting your address

You can restore a deleted address for <Value of="mailboxDeleteGraceDays" /> days, like any other mailbox (see
[Mailboxes](/docs/mailboxes#delete-a-mailbox)); after that it and its mail are gone for good. The name stays retired:
your account can take it again later, nobody else can.

## If we created it for you

Our staff can create your free address when you ask for help. They never choose its password: the account's owner
sets it from the mailbox's **Password** tab. It's recorded in your account's [audit log](/docs/audit-log) as an
Anhourtec staff action.
